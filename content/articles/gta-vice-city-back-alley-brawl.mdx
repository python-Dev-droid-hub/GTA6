“Back Alley Brawl” is one of the early missions in Grand Theft Auto: Vice City. Tommy Vercetti receives the job from Kent Paul, who sends him to find a chef connected to the music scene. The mission introduces players to close combat, firearms, and Vice City's police system.

If you are struggling to complete it, this blog explains how to pass the Back Alley Brawl mission step by step.

## What Is the Back Alley Brawl Mission?

“Back Alley Brawl” is an early story mission given by Kent Paul. You can start it at the Malibu Club after progressing through the opening missions.

Kent Paul asks Tommy to locate a chef who may know more about the mysterious people behind the deal that went wrong. Tommy travels to a back alley, confronts the chef, and then follows a lead involving a mobile phone.

The mission is fairly short, but knowing where to go and when to fight makes it much easier.

## How to Start Back Alley Brawl?

Before starting the mission, make sure you have access to the Malibu Club area and can trigger Kent Paul's mission.

When you arrive at the mission marker, approach Kent Paul. A cutscene begins, and he explains what Tommy needs to do.

After the conversation, the mission starts.

Your main objectives are:

- Find the chef.
- Defeat him and his associates.
- Take the chef's phone.
- Follow Lance Vance.
- Return after completing the objective.

## Step 1: Drive to the Back Alley

After the cutscene with Kent Paul, get into a vehicle and follow the mission marker.

The location is not far away, so you do not need to drive aggressively. Follow the GPS or map marker and park near the alley.

Once you arrive, Tommy will encounter the chef.

## Step 2: Defeat the Chef

The chef and his associates will attack Tommy.

You can use your fists if you want to save ammunition. However, having a weapon makes the fight easier, especially if several enemies attack at once.

Try not to stand in the middle of the group. Move around and deal with enemies one at a time.

### Combat Tips

- Keep moving during the fight.
- Avoid letting several enemies surround Tommy.
- Use a firearm if you have one available.
- Pick up dropped weapons after defeating enemies.
- Watch your health during close combat.
- Do not waste ammunition on enemies who are already down.

Once the chef is defeated, Tommy can pick up the phone.

## Step 3: Take the Chef's Phone

After the fight, approach the phone that the chef drops.

Tommy will interact with it, and another character will appear. This introduces Lance Vance, who becomes an important character later in the story.

Follow Lance after the conversation.

At this point, you do not need to start another major fight. Focus on staying close and following the mission progression.

## Step 4: Follow Lance Vance

Lance will leave the area in a vehicle.

Get into your own vehicle and follow him.

Do not worry about catching him at high speed. Keep enough distance to avoid crashing into traffic or losing control of your vehicle.

Following Lance completes the final part of the mission.

## How to Easily Pass Back Alley Brawl?

The mission itself is straightforward, but a few simple strategies can make it easier.

### Get a Weapon Before Starting

Having a firearm can make the first fight much quicker. You can obtain weapons during your earlier progress through Vice City.

If you prefer melee combat, the mission can still be completed without relying heavily on firearms.

### Keep Your Health High

Do not begin the mission with very low health. The fight in the alley can quickly reduce Tommy's health if several enemies attack together.

If necessary, collect health before triggering the mission.

### Use the Environment

The alley gives you limited space, so positioning matters. Try to avoid getting trapped against a wall.

Move backward when enemies approach and create enough space to attack safely.

### Follow the Map

Once the fighting is over, the mission becomes mostly about reaching the correct locations. Use the map marker instead of driving around randomly.

### Avoid Unnecessary Police Attention

You do not need a wanted level to complete this mission. Avoid attacking pedestrians, stealing police vehicles, or causing unnecessary destruction while traveling between objectives.

## Common Problems During Back Alley Brawl

### I Cannot Find the Chef

Check the mission marker and make sure you have entered the correct alley. The chef is located in the marked area.

### The Chef Keeps Attacking Me

Use a weapon or keep moving while fighting. Standing still makes it easier for multiple enemies to hit Tommy.

### I Lost Lance

If you lose track of Lance, check the map and follow the mission marker. Avoid taking unnecessary shortcuts that can cause you to lose him.

### I Died During the Fight

Try starting the mission with full health and a weapon. During the fight, deal with the closest threats first rather than rushing toward the entire group.

## Is Back Alley Brawl Difficult?

For most players, Back Alley Brawl is an easy early-game mission. It mainly teaches basic combat and introduces Lance Vance.

The biggest challenge is usually the initial fight. Once you defeat the chef and understand the mission route, the remaining objectives are simple.

## What Happens After Back Alley Brawl?

Completing “Back Alley Brawl” allows you to continue progressing through the early Vice City storyline.

The mission also helps establish Lance Vance as an important part of Tommy's story. As you complete more missions, the relationship between Tommy and the other major characters becomes increasingly important.

## Final Tips to Complete Back Alley Brawl

Before starting the mission, make sure Tommy has enough health and, if possible, a useful weapon. Follow Kent Paul's instructions, reach the alley, defeat the chef and his associates, collect the phone, and follow Lance.

The key is not to rush the first fight. Keep moving, avoid being surrounded, and use the map to follow the remaining objectives. Once you complete these steps, “Back Alley Brawl” should be an easy mission to pass in GTA Vice City.
