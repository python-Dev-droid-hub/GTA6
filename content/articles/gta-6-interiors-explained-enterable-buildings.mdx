For years, Grand Theft Auto fans have faced the same frustration: driving down a neon-lit Vice City street, pulling up to a massive building, and being greeted by a static texture and locked doors. While GTA 5 delivered a massive open world, the vast majority of its structures served as non-interactive backdrops.

With Grand Theft Auto VI, Rockstar Games is completely reshaping how we interact with the map. Thanks to modern hardware capabilities on the PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, the state of Leonida is built with environmental depth in mind.

Here is a breakdown of what we know about GTA 6 interiors, how many buildings you can enter, and which specific locations you can explore.

## How Many Buildings Can You Enter in GTA 6?

Rockstar Games has confirmed that GTA 6 will feature hundreds of enterable interiors across Vice City and surrounding regions. Rather than treating interiors as mission-only set pieces, the developer is integrating them directly into free-roam gameplay, robbery systems, and ambient world design.

Community leaks and mapping projects suggest that over 700 commercial and residential buildings could feature fully modeled interiors, with reports pointing to up to 40% of large-scale structures being accessible. While hard numbers outside of Rockstar's official statements remain unverified, official gameplay captures confirm a massive jump in accessibility compared to previous titles.

## What Types of Buildings Can Players Explore?

GTA 6 expands Vice City's world with unprecedented interior accessibility. Players can freely enter convenience stores, gas stations, multi-level shopping malls, night clubs, dive bars, gyms, and bank vaults. These seamless, load-free interiors enhance gameplay, offering deep opportunities for stealth scouting, interactive minigames, dynamic police pursuits, and high-stakes robberies.

### Robbing & Scouting: Small Commercial Businesses

Convenience stores and gas station back-offices are no longer simple drop-in points for quick cash. In GTA 6, players can actively scout interiors before acting. You can check security camera setups, judge guard counts, disarm silent alarms behind counter spaces, or pick locks to enter storage rooms after hours.

### Multi-Level Complexes & Malls

Venues like Shoreline Market (inspired by Miami's Bayside Marketplace) and Gator Plaza showcase multi-level interior design. Players can move smoothly between parking lots, exterior walkways, food courts, and individual retail fronts without traditional loading screens getting in the way.

### Nightclubs, Bars, & Social Hubs

Nightlife is a central pillar of Vice City's identity. Venues such as NINE1NINE and The Vault feature dense crowd physics, fully rendered dance floors, VIP zones, and back-office areas tied to story contacts and side activities.

### Interactive Services (Gyms, Salons, & Banks)

Unlike GTA 5, where physical stat progression was limited, GTA 6 brings back interactive service locations. Gym interiors allow Lucia and Jason to use treadmills, free weights, and fitness equipment to tweak physical stats. Barber shops, salons, and bank vaults also feature walk-in transitions rather than quick menu overlays.

## Will Interiors Have Loading Screens?

Official footage from the game confirms that interior-to-exterior transitions occur seamlessly with no visible loading screens. Whether you are running from police through a hotel lobby, escaping out a back fire exit, or slipping into a neighborhood bar to lose your wanted level, the world streams continuously around you.

## Final Thoughts

Grand Theft Auto VI marks a revolutionary shift in open-world design by transforming Vice City from a collection of decorative facades into a living, fully accessible ecosystem. With hundreds of seamless, load-free interiors—ranging from bustling shopping malls to high-stakes bank vaults—Rockstar Games is delivering unprecedented depth. This dramatic increase in enterable buildings ensures that every alleyway, venue, and storefront offers genuine opportunities for exploration, stealth, and chaos.
