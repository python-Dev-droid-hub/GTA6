Vehicles have always played a major role in the Grand Theft Auto series. They help players explore the map, escape police chases, complete missions, and enjoy the freedom of an open-world game. In Grand Theft Auto VI (GTA 6), vehicle variety is especially exciting because the game takes players across Vice City and the wider state of Leonida.

Official promotional materials have revealed a range of vehicles, including cars, motorcycles, boats, and aircraft. Rockstar Games has also highlighted custom builds, vehicle-related locations, and classic automotive designs. However, some gameplay details, such as complete vehicle statistics and ownership systems, remain unconfirmed.

## What Vehicles Can Players Expect in GTA 6?

GTA 6 is expected to offer a varied vehicle selection suited to urban streets, coastal highways, rural roads, and wetlands. Official materials have already identified several named vehicles and shown different forms of transportation. The complete launch roster and every vehicle's performance specifications have not been fully documented by Rockstar.

The most interesting categories include:

- Sports Cars and Supercars
- Muscle Cars and Classic Vehicles
- Motorcycles and Dirt Bikes
- SUVs and Pickup Trucks
- Boats and Personal Watercraft
- Aircraft
- Off-Road Vehicles

## 1. Sports Cars and Supercars Worth Watching

Sports cars are among the most exciting vehicles to watch in GTA 6. Vice City features a modern urban setting where fast cars fit naturally into busy roads, upscale neighborhoods, and nighttime street scenes.

One officially named example is the ’95 Grotti Cheetah, featured in Rockstar's Ultimate Edition promotional materials. Its classic styling makes it an interesting option for players who enjoy collecting distinctive vehicles.

Beyond individual models, several aspects of sports car gameplay deserve attention.

## 2. Muscle Cars and Classic Vehicles

Classic cars are another major attraction in the GTA 6 vehicles lineup. Their styling fits the broader atmosphere of Vice City, where modern luxury, nostalgic design, and car culture can coexist.

Rockstar has named several vehicles and vehicle-related features in its promotional materials, including the ’55 Vapid Stanier Sedan, the Ganado Retro Build, and the ’67 Vapid Dominator Buggy. These examples demonstrate an interest in different automotive styles rather than a lineup focused exclusively on modern supercars.

Classic and muscle-inspired vehicles are worth watching for three reasons:

- Distinctive Designs
- Customization Potential
- Different Driving Characteristics

## 3. Motorcycles and Dirt Bikes

Motorcycles provide a different way to explore an open-world game. Their smaller size can make them useful in traffic, while dirt bikes offer a natural fit for rough terrain and less developed roads.

Official GTA 6 promotional footage includes motorcycles, and Rockstar's Ultimate Edition materials name the Dinka Enduro motorcycle among Jason's safehouse vehicles.

Several motorcycle features deserve attention:

- Traffic Navigation
- Off-Road Handling
- Rider Animations
- Vehicle Damage
- Police Pursuits

## 4. Boats, Jet Skis, and Water Transportation

Water transportation is especially relevant to GTA 6 because Leonida includes coastal environments and waterways. Boats could allow players to move between waterfront locations, explore open water, and approach destinations from different directions.

Rockstar has officially named the Shitzu Squalo, a powerboat, and the Crest Kayak in its promotional materials. The kayak is particularly interesting because it offers a different type of water travel from a motorized boat.

Water-based vehicles are worth watching for several reasons.

### Coastal Exploration

Boats could make beaches, islands, marinas, and other waterfront areas easier to explore. Their usefulness will depend on the game's map design and which areas are accessible.

### Water-Based Police Chases

Marine pursuits could create a different challenge from road chases. Players may need to navigate narrow waterways, avoid obstacles, or find suitable places to leave the water. The precise pursuit mechanics remain unconfirmed.

### Different Types of Watercraft

Motorboats prioritize powered travel, while kayaks suggest slower, manually controlled movement. If their handling and accessibility differ substantially, players could have more than one reason to explore Leonida's waterways.

## 5. Planes and Helicopters

Aircraft can change how players approach a large open-world map. Instead of traveling along roads, players may be able to cover long distances by air, observe locations from above, or reach destinations that are difficult to access by land.

Official GTA 6 footage has shown aircraft, including helicopters and planes. However, the complete list of flyable aircraft, their availability, and their performance characteristics should not be assumed from brief appearances alone.

The most important features to watch include:

- Flight Controls
- Aircraft Handling
- Travel Convenience
- Mission Integration
- Aircraft Access

## 6. SUVs, Pickup Trucks, and Off-Road Vehicles

Not every journey requires a supercar. SUVs, pickups, and rugged vehicles may be better suited to rural roads, uneven ground, and the swampy environments beyond Vice City's urban center.

The ’67 Vapid Dominator Buggy is an officially named off-road vehicle featured in Rockstar's promotional materials. Its rugged design makes it one of the more interesting vehicles to watch for players who enjoy exploring beyond paved roads.

Off-road transportation deserves attention because Leonida is not limited to city streets.

### Terrain and Traction

Mud, grass, dirt, and uneven surfaces can make driving more challenging if the game gives these conditions a meaningful effect on vehicle handling.

### Vehicle Durability

Rugged vehicles may suit rough environments, but their actual durability and resistance to damage should not be assumed without confirmed gameplay evidence.

### Exploring Rural Leonida

Pickup trucks, SUVs, and off-road vehicles could help players navigate areas away from the main urban roads. They may also provide a different experience from low-slung sports cars.

## 7. Vehicle Customization and Garages

Vehicle customization is worth watching because it can turn ordinary transportation into something personal. Players often enjoy changing a vehicle's appearance, collecting different builds, and creating a garage that reflects their preferred driving style.

Rockstar's promotional materials feature custom vehicle builds, including the Ganado Retro Build, as well as the Rideout Customs mod shop. These are concrete signs that customized vehicles and vehicle-related locations have a place in GTA 6's promotional content.

However, the complete customization system has not been confirmed.

Potentially important features include:

- Paint and Liveries
- Wheels and Bodywork
- Performance Upgrades
- Garage Management
- Vehicle Restoration

## 8. Police Pursuits and Vehicle-Based Gameplay

Vehicles become more interesting when the game gives players reasons to use them strategically. Police pursuits are a natural example because a player's choice of transportation can influence how an escape unfolds.

A fast sports car may be useful on open roads, while a motorcycle could offer more flexibility in congested areas. A boat might become relevant near waterways if the game's pursuit systems support marine escapes.

The key features worth watching include:

- Vehicle Choice
- Handling Under Pressure
- Damage During Chases
- Environmental Routes
- Police Transportation

## What Should Players Watch Before GTA 6 Launches?

Before launch, players should focus on official footage and announcements rather than relying on unverified vehicle lists or performance claims.

Start by checking Rockstar Games' official GTA VI website and trailers. Look for named vehicles, recognizable vehicle categories, modification locations, and scenes that demonstrate transportation in action.

Next, compare what appears in promotional footage with what Rockstar explicitly confirms. A vehicle visible in a trailer may be identifiable by its design, but its in-game name, exact specifications, and availability may remain unknown.

Finally, pay attention to details that affect actual gameplay. Vehicle handling, customization, accessibility, terrain interaction, and the relationship between transportation and missions are more useful indicators than a vehicle count alone.

The goal is to understand how transportation supports the overall game, not just to build the longest possible list of cars.

## Final Thoughts

GTA 6 vehicles promise an exciting mix of sports cars, motorcycles, boats, aircraft, and off-road rides. Vehicle customization, handling, and police pursuits are key features worth watching. As launch approaches, follow official Rockstar Games updates to discover confirmed vehicles and transportation features that could shape your Leonida adventure.
