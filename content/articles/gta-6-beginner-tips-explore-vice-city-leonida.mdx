Stepping into the sun-drenched, chaotic state of Leonida for the first time can feel overwhelming. Between the neon-lit streets of Vice City, the winding bridges of the Leonida Keys, and the gator-filled swamps of the Grassrivers, Rockstar’s latest open world is packed with systems that reward smart planning over reckless chaos.

Whether you are jumping in at launch or preparing for your first session, start by dialing in the best [GTA 6 PS5 settings](/news/gta-6-ps5-settings-guide), then use these [GTA 6](/) beginner tips to survive early gunfights, keep the police off your back, and explore Leonida like a seasoned pro.

## Finish the Opening Missions Before Free-Roaming

It is tempting to steal the nearest sports car and immediately drive across the state, but sticking to the early story objectives pays off. The opening missions serve as a natural tutorial that introduces core mechanics—like switching between protagonists Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos, using the weapon and item wheels, and managing vehicle storage. Completing these initial chapters also unlocks basic safe houses and essential gear so you aren't heading into the open world empty-handed.

## Outsmart the Upgraded Police System

Law enforcement in Leonida is much sharper than in previous entries. Rather than relying solely on line-of-sight chases, officers track descriptions of your clothing, vehicle make, and visible details tied to a crime scene.

### Swap Outfits and Masks

Law enforcement officers in Leonida remember exactly what you wear during a heist or street crime. Always equip a mask before starting an illegal job to conceal your identity from witnesses and security cameras. Once you escape immediate police sightlines, duck into a clothing store or safe house to change outfits, instantly lowering your wanted recognition level.

### Ditch Hot Vehicles

Driving a recognizable getaway car through Vice City is the fastest way to attract unwanted police attention. After losing pursuing cruisers, abandon your flagged vehicle inside a covered parking garage, narrow alleyway, or wooded trail. Steal a clean, unmarked car from a nearby street so patrolling officers cannot match your license plate or vehicle description to the crime.

### Use Focus Mode

Activating Focus mode gives you a critical tactical advantage when planning escapes or evading active police searches. This mechanic slows down your perception of the environment, highlighting nearby law enforcement sightlines, patrol routes, and hidden alleyways. Trigger Focus mode before turning blind corners so you can slip past searching officers unnoticed and keep your wanted level from escalating.

## Master Driving Physics and Vehicle Management

Vehicle handling in GTA 6 features weightier physics and distinct differences across car classes. Take time to practice braking and cornering in traffic before attempting high-speed pursuits—especially since overturned cars can no longer be magically flipped upright just by steering.

Keep an eye on your fuel gauge as well. Combustion engines require stops at gas stations, while electric vehicles need charging stations. When you want to reach a distant waypoint without worrying about fuel or traffic, open the RydeMe app on your in-game phone to hail a driver.

## Organize Your Loadout and Trunk Storage

Owning a weapon and carrying it on your person are two different things. Your personal carry capacity is limited, meaning your vehicle’s trunk acts as your mobile armory. Before starting a mission or entering a high-risk area:

- Inspect both your Loadout and Storage menus at your vehicle.
- Practice navigating the item wheel (R1 on PS5 / RB on Xbox) in a quiet area so you can quickly grab healing items and body armor during a shootout.
- Use solid cover during gunfights rather than standing in the open.

## Explore Every Region Beyond Vice City

While Vice City is the massive, Miami-inspired urban centerpiece, some of the best encounters, side activities, and smuggling routes sit outside city limits. Make time to visit all six major regions of Leonida:

### Leonida Keys

Connected by long, sun-bleached causeways, the Leonida Keys offer a stunning tropical escape south of Vice City. This island chain is the ultimate playground for high-speed boat racing, deep-sea diving, and unearthing hidden coastal contraband. Watch out for local marine patrols and unpredictable weather rolling in off the Atlantic during your island excursions.

### Grassrivers

Spanning vast, murky swamplains inspired by the Everglades, the Grassrivers region is a rugged wilderness built for exploration. Trade your sports car for an airboat to navigate shallow waters, tracking rare wildlife and remote hideouts. Always stay alert—submerged gators and territorial locals make traversing these untamed wetlands a dangerous yet thrilling adventure.

### Port Gellhorn

A faded industrial and resort town on Leonida’s Gulf coast, Port Gellhorn serves as a gritty hub for local hustles and petty crime. Lined with rusted boardwalks, cheap motels, and pawn shops, it provides a stark contrast to Vice City's neon lights. It is an ideal area for picking up early-game odd jobs and cheap gear.

### Ambrosia

Ambrosia forms Leonida’s rugged agricultural and industrial heartland. Dominated by expansive sugar fields, quiet farmhouses, and rough biker outposts, this rural territory requires careful navigation. The open roads invite high-speed driving, but local law enforcement and hostile crews patrol closely.

### Mount Kalaga National Park

Rising across the northern highlands, Mount Kalaga National Park delivers towering forests, steep ridges, and scenic off-road trails. It is the premier destination for hunting, fishing, and testing rugged all-terrain vehicles far away from city congestion.

## Spend Your Starting Cash Wisely

Early money is tight, and flashy cosmetic upgrades or luxury cars won't keep you alive. Prioritize spending on practical upgrades—ammunition, body armor, medical supplies, and essential weapons—to solve immediate problems. Hold off on expensive real estate until mid-game once you have reliable income streams running.

## Save Manually Before Taking Big Risks

Leonida is unpredictable, and random world events can escalate fast. Always check the pause menu and create a manual save before pulling off an unscripted robbery, testing a risky shortcut, or logging off for the day.

## Final Thoughts

Mastering Leonida requires a blend of smart planning, tactical survival, and sharp situational awareness. By managing your cash wisely, evading the law through outfit changes and clean getaways, and exploring every corner of Vice City, you will conquer Rockstar's massive open world with ease. Gear up, stay alert, and enjoy your journey across the state of Leonida.
