Rather than giving each protagonist a separate superpower like Grand Theft Auto V, [GTA 6](/) builds its gameplay differences around fighting styles, individual stat tracking, teamwork dynamics, and personal Criminal Profiles for Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos. Both characters share the core Focus mechanic, yet how they fight, build stats, and respond to the world of Leonida creates distinct tactical choices.

## Shared Core Ability: How Focus Works

Both Jason and Lucia use Focus, a mechanic that blends slow-motion gunplay with environmental awareness. Activating Focus slows down time and adds a tactical overlay to combat and exploration.

### Lethal vs. Non-Lethal Targeting

During Focus, GTA 6 highlights weak points on targets so you can pick your approach mid-fight. Red markers point to lethal spots for fast takedowns in tough firefights, while yellow markers highlight non-lethal spots to incapacitate enemies without killing them. Choosing between lethal force and non-lethal control shapes how fights play out and affects your Criminal Profile.

### Vehicle Weak Points

Focus also spotlights key parts on moving vehicles during high-speed chases and ambushes. Targeting engines, fuel tanks, or tires lets you slow down rivals, force stalls, or disable police cruisers before they box you in. Picking the right vehicle weak point gives you cleaner escapes and sharper control when traffic and gunfire get chaotic.

### World & Police Awareness

Activating Focus turns the HUD into a quick tactical scan of your surroundings across Leonida. It highlights nearby valuables, interactive objects, local events, and police positions so you can spot threats and opportunities faster. Using Focus before moving or breaking cover helps you read sightlines, avoid patrols, and plan your next move.

### Meter & Recovery

Using Focus drains its meter, so pacing your slow-motion bursts matters during extended gunfights or chases. You can top up or boost the meter with consumables, specific food and drinks, washing up, or stealth and non-lethal takedowns. Smart meter management keeps Focus ready for tense moments instead of running dry mid-encounter.

## Jason Duval vs. Lucia Caminos: Key Gameplay Differences

While both protagonists access the same core systems, their backgrounds and combat profiles shape how they handle encounters.

| Gameplay Aspect | Jason Duval | Lucia Caminos |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Background | Ex-military and former drug runner with a cautious, reserved approach. | Amateur fighter released from prison at the start of the story with a bold, expressive edge. |
| Melee Style | Heavy street brawler focused on raw power and hard-hitting strikes. | Technical, agile fighter relying on kickboxing and MMA movement. |
| Stats & Progression | Tracks Strength, Cardio, and Focus on an individual profile. | Tracks Strength, Cardio, and Focus on a separate individual profile. |
| Criminal Profile | Maintains an independent standing that shifts based on your actions as Jason. | Maintains an independent standing that shifts based on your actions as Lucia. |

## Fighting Styles and Combat Flow

Melee combat is one of the clearest distinctions between the two leads. Jason approaches close-quarters fights like a heavy brawler throwing powerful blows, while Lucia leans into faster, more technical kickboxing and MMA movements. Across gunfights and close encounters, players also contend with tighter weapon carrying—relying on personal vehicles to store extra gear—alongside crouching, going prone, dodging, countering, and grabbing.

## Individual Stats and Gym Training

Both characters develop three core stats—Strength (maximum health), Cardio (maximum stamina), and Focus (slow-motion duration)—viewed in the Jason & Lucia menu. Because stats are tracked per character, training only one protagonist in solo play leaves the other behind unless you train together.

## Separate Criminal Profiles

Each character has their own Criminal Profile, which can be checked from the menu or via a quick HUD prompt. Playstyle choices—such as stealthy, non-lethal takedowns versus aggressive or reckless acts—shape each character's rating independently, meaning one protagonist can stay controlled while the other goes down a rougher path. Quiet approaches are covered in the [GTA 6 Stealth Gameplay Guide](/news/gta-6-stealth-gameplay-guide).

## Switching and Teamwork Mechanics

GTA 6 turns dual protagonists into a coordinated partnership rather than isolated solo runs. Switching between Jason and Lucia is quick—instant when they are close together, or zooming out to drop into the other's location across Leonida when apart, subject to mission rules. For controls and Partner Freeroam tips, see the [GTA 6 Character Switching Guide](/news/gta-6-character-switching-guide).

### Two-Person Heists and Robberies

Pairing Jason and Lucia turns robberies and heists into coordinated two-person jobs across Leonida. One protagonist can cover angles or hold targets in place while the other sweeps cash, cracks safes, or handles crowd control. Switching roles on the fly helps you adapt fast when alarms trip or backup arrives unexpectedly. Store-tier holdups are detailed in [Can You Rob Stores in GTA 6?](/news/can-you-rob-stores-in-gta-6).

### Driving and Shooting

Chases and getaways feel more dynamic when you can trade driving and shooting duties between Jason and Lucia. One character can hold a steady line through traffic while the other aims out the window at pursuing vehicles or tires. Contextual prompts also let you push the pace during tight escapes.

### Shared Gains and Splitting Heat

Working together as a pair carries clear practical payoffs during jobs, training, and post-crime escapes. Running activities and training side by side builds shared progression and stat gains, while splitting up after a score helps shake police heat faster. Smart positioning keeps both characters safer when pressure rises.

### Relationship Levels

Spending time together, doing side activities, and texting shapes Jason and Lucia's relationship across four tiers, from Running on Empty to Hot Together. Stronger chemistry supports smoother teamwork in the field, giving your partnership a clear personal arc alongside the action.

## Conclusion

The real difference in GTA 6 gameplay comes from how you pair Jason and Lucia's strengths rather than relying on one-off gimmicks. Blending Jason's heavy brawler edge and cautious setup with Lucia's agile striking and bold momentum—while managing their shared Focus, separate stats, and Criminal Profiles—gives you flexible ways to tackle every job across Leonida.
