GTA Vice City “An Old Friend” is the opening sequence that introduces Tommy Vercetti to Vice City and sets the story in motion. Unlike later missions, it is not a combat-heavy mission. Instead, it focuses on reaching a safe location, meeting Ken Rosenberg, and beginning Tommy’s new life in the city.

If you are starting a new game and want to know how to complete “An Old Friend” in GTA Vice City, this blog explains every step.

## What Is the “An Old Friend” Mission in GTA Vice City?

“An Old Friend” is the introductory mission of GTA Vice City. It begins immediately after the opening cutscene, where Tommy Vercetti arrives in Vice City following his release from prison.

Tommy meets his lawyer, Ken Rosenberg, at the airport. After a short conversation, Ken tells Tommy about the deal that has been arranged and sends him toward the next location.

The mission mainly serves as a tutorial for driving around Vice City and introduces several important characters and locations.

## How to Start “An Old Friend”?

You do not need to complete any previous mission to start this mission.

After the opening cutscene:

- Tommy arrives at Escobar International Airport.
- Walk toward the waiting vehicle.
- Get into the car.
- Follow the route to Ken Rosenberg's office.
- Park near the marked destination.
- Enter the building to trigger the next scene.

The game gives you a basic introduction to movement and driving during this section.

## Step-by-Step Guide to Complete “An Old Friend”

### 1. Leave the Airport

After the opening scene, Tommy is at the airport. Get into the vehicle waiting nearby.

You can use the map to identify your destination. Follow the road signs and waypoint until you reach Ken Rosenberg's office.

There is no need to search for a special vehicle or weapon during this part.

### 2. Drive to Ken Rosenberg's Office

Drive through Vice City toward Ken's office. Take your time if you are unfamiliar with the city's roads.

Avoid unnecessary crashes because damaged vehicles can make driving more difficult. You also do not need to fight pedestrians or police officers.

Simply follow the destination marker shown on the radar.

### 3. Reach the Destination

When you arrive at Ken Rosenberg's office, park the vehicle in the designated area.

A short cutscene will begin when Tommy reaches the correct location. This conversation provides important story information and introduces Ken Rosenberg as one of Tommy's contacts in Vice City.

### 4. Watch the Cutscene

Tommy and Ken discuss the failed deal and the situation surrounding Tommy's arrival in Vice City.

The scene establishes the main conflict that drives the early part of the game. Tommy needs to find out who betrayed him and recover the money and drugs that were lost during the deal.

Once the conversation ends, the introductory mission is complete.

## Is “An Old Friend” Difficult to Complete?

No. “An Old Friend” is one of the easiest missions in GTA Vice City.

There are no major shootouts, difficult chases, or complicated objectives. The main challenge is simply learning how to navigate Vice City and reach the destination.

If you are playing GTA Vice City for the first time, use the radar to keep track of your destination and avoid unnecessary distractions.

## Tips for Completing “An Old Friend”

### Follow the Radar

The radar is your main navigation tool. Keep an eye on the destination marker while driving.

### Avoid Unnecessary Trouble

There is no reason to attack pedestrians, steal multiple vehicles, or attract police attention during this mission. Staying focused makes the objective straightforward.

### Learn the Streets

This mission gives you an early opportunity to become familiar with Vice City's roads. Pay attention to major streets and landmarks because later missions require you to travel across the city frequently.

### Take Care While Driving

You do not need to drive at maximum speed. A controlled drive is safer and helps you reach the destination without unnecessary problems.

## What Happens After “An Old Friend”?

After completing “An Old Friend,” Tommy's story in Vice City begins to develop.

Ken Rosenberg becomes one of Tommy's early contacts, while Tommy starts looking for answers about the failed deal. The events that follow introduce more characters, missions, businesses, and rival criminal organizations.

From this point, you can begin working through the main storyline and gradually build Tommy's influence across Vice City.

## Final Thoughts

“An Old Friend” is a simple introduction to GTA Vice City and does not require advanced driving or combat skills. Your main objective is to leave the airport, drive to Ken Rosenberg's office, and complete the introductory sequence.

Follow the radar, avoid unnecessary trouble, and reach the marked destination. Once the cutscene finishes, the mission is complete and Tommy Vercetti's journey through Vice City officially begins.
