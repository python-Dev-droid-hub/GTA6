When players step into the sun-soaked, neon-lit state of Leonida in Grand Theft Auto VI, the main storyline featuring Jason and Lucia is only the tip of the iceberg. Rockstar Games has built their largest, most immersive map to date—stretching far beyond the bustling streets of Vice City into dense wetlands, forgotten industrial zones, and treacherous mountain ranges.

If you put down the story missions and venture off the beaten path, Leonida reveals a web of hidden easter eggs, restricted zones, side activities, and secret gear waiting to be uncovered.

Here is your ultimate blog to exploring GTA 6’s hidden locations and secrets beyond the main story.

## Top Hidden Locations in Leonida You Must Visit

While the map radar highlights major highways and cities, many of Leonida’s best-kept secrets remain completely unmarked. Pack your gear and check out these off-the-grid locations:

### Mount Kalaga Military Bunker

Deep within the backwoods of Lummox County lies Mount Kalaga National Park. Tucked against the western slopes is a heavy-security military bunker. If you manage to bypass the perimeter defense (or secure a keycard from Port Gellhorn), you can infiltrate the underground research facilities to steal high-tier tactical gear and rare military prototypes.

### Smugglers’ Island (Off the Coast of the Keys)

Just south of the Leonida Keys archipelago lies an unmarked island surrounded by shallow coral reefs and sandbars. Take a jet ski or small speedboat out to find the wreckage of a twin-engine cargo plane filled with abandoned cash caches and elite armor.

### Cypress River Caverns

Tucked along the northern river gorges, this subterranean cave network requires players to navigate pitch-black tunnels using flashlights or night-vision gear. Swim through underwater caverns to discover rare historical loot—including a hidden pirate sword.

### Downtown Vice City Abandoned Metro Tunnels

Beneath the glittering skyscrapers of Vice City is an abandoned subterranean transit network. Walking the tracks off the central line leads to hidden panic rooms and black-market dealers selling illegal modifications.

## Secrets Hidden Underwater: Scuba Diving & Shipwrecks

Rockstar upgraded underwater mechanics significantly for GTA 6. Equipping scuba gear—masks, flippers, and oxygen tanks—opens up an entirely separate ecosystem off the coast of Vice-Dale and Leonard Counties:

### Drowned Cargo Ships

Dive deep along the eastern coastline to locate sunken freight ships from past smuggling runs. Lockboxes containing gold bullion and high-value commodities are scattered around the sea floor.

### Submerged Research Camps & Ruins

Exploration in the murky mangrove swamps of the Grassrivers leads to submerged ruins and flooded research outposts hidden under floating algae blooms.

## Tips for Exploring Leonida Safely

Exploring Leonida’s uncharted wilderness carries serious risks. From territorial wildlife in the Grassrivers to heavily armed patrols around restricted bunkers, danger is everywhere. Always pack adequate body armor, carry high-capacity firearms, keep healing supplies ready, and secure a fast escape vehicle before stepping off the beaten path into unknown territory.

### Use the Right Vehicles

Navigating Leonida requires matching your transport to the terrain. Deep-draft speedboats easily ground themselves on shallow sandbars throughout the Keys, making agile jet skis ideal for offshore exploration. Meanwhile, dense, marshy Grassrivers demand airboats to skim over algae and mud. Choosing the right vehicle ensures you reach hidden locations quickly without getting stranded in dangerous, predator-infested waters.

### Dynamic Weather Triggers

Leonida’s dynamic weather system directly influences how you discover secrets. Heavy tropical downpours raise inland water levels, submerging low-lying pathways while opening deep river gaps for boats. Conversely, low tides uncover hidden sea caves and sunken wreckage along the coast. Pay close attention to shifting weather patterns, as certain mysterious locations only become accessible during specific atmospheric conditions.

### Keep an Eye Out for Environmental Cues

Unmarked secrets will rarely trigger radar markers until you stand right on top of them. Instead, rely on visual clues embedded in the world. Look for broken fences, tire tracks veering into dense foliage, subtle cave openings behind waterfalls, or flickering lights inside seemingly abandoned structures. Rockstar heavily rewards observant players who pay close attention to subtle environment details.

## Final Thoughts

Exploring Leonida beyond the main story unlocks GTA 6’s richest secrets, rare loot, and unforgettable adventures. Whether you are diving for sunken shipwrecks, infiltrating off-the-grid bunkers, or surviving underground fight clubs, stepping off the beaten path pays off immensely. Gear up, pay close attention to environmental cues, and choose your vehicles wisely. Countless mysteries are waiting across Vice City and beyond—go out and claim them!
