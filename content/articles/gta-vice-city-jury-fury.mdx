If you are playing GTA Vice City and have reached the “Jury Fury” mission, you may find its objective confusing at first. Unlike many missions that require you to reach a destination and eliminate specific enemies, this mission focuses on intimidating two jurors.

In “Jury Fury,” Tommy Vercetti is asked to scare two people who are involved in a case against a client. You do not need to kill them. Instead, you must damage their vehicles and use intimidation to make them change their attitude.

This blog explains how to start the mission, find both jurors, complete each objective, and avoid common mistakes.

## What Is the “Jury Fury” Mission in GTA Vice City?

“Jury Fury” is an early story mission given to Tommy Vercetti by Ken Rosenberg. It becomes available after completing “Riot” and is part of the missions connected to Rosenberg's law office.

The main objective is simple: intimidate two jurors by damaging their cars.

You do not need special weapons or a fast vehicle to complete the mission. A regular car and a little patience are enough.

## How to Start “Jury Fury”?

To begin the mission, travel to Ken Rosenberg's office in Vice Point. Look for the pink mission marker outside the building.

Enter the marker to trigger the cutscene. Ken explains that two jurors need some convincing. Tommy then takes responsibility for intimidating them.

Once the conversation ends, the mission begins.

## “Jury Fury” Mission Objectives

The mission can be summarized in a few simple steps:

- Start the mission at Ken Rosenberg's office.
- Find the first juror.
- Damage the juror's vehicle.
- Make the juror leave the area.
- Locate the second juror.
- Damage the second juror's vehicle.
- Intimidate the second juror.
- Complete the mission and return to the story.

The key point is that you are supposed to damage the vehicles rather than kill the jurors.

## How to Complete the First Juror Objective?

After the mission starts, head toward the location shown by the mission marker.

You will find the first juror near his vehicle. Your goal is to make his car take enough damage to frighten him.

You can use your vehicle to ram the juror's car repeatedly. Drive into different parts of the vehicle instead of hitting the same area continuously. This helps you damage it while keeping control of your own car.

As the vehicle becomes damaged, the juror will react and eventually leave.

### Avoid Running Over the Juror

It can be tempting to drive directly into the juror. However, killing the target can cause problems because the mission is based on intimidation.

Keep your attention on the vehicle, not the person.

A controlled approach makes this part much easier.

## How to Find the Second Juror?

Once the first juror has been intimidated, the game will direct you toward the second target.

Follow the new mission marker and locate the second juror. He will also be near his vehicle.

The basic strategy remains the same. Use your vehicle to damage the target's car.

You do not need to use an expensive vehicle. A standard car is usually enough to complete the objective.

## How to Intimidate the Second Juror?

The second juror may require more damage before the game recognizes that you have completed the objective.

Keep ramming the vehicle carefully until it becomes heavily damaged. Watch your own vehicle's condition as well. If your car becomes badly damaged, find another vehicle and continue.

Once the second juror has been properly intimidated, the mission objective will be completed.

## Can You Use Weapons in “Jury Fury”?

Weapons are generally unnecessary for this mission.

The easiest method is to use a vehicle because the mission specifically revolves around damaging the jurors' cars. Using a gun can create unnecessary problems, including killing the target or attracting police attention.

For a straightforward completion, stick with vehicle attacks.

## Tips for Completing “Jury Fury” Easily

### 1. Use a Strong Vehicle

A durable vehicle can make the mission easier because you need to ram other cars several times.

Avoid using a badly damaged vehicle when starting the mission.

### 2. Focus on the Cars

Remember that the objective is intimidation. Damage the vehicles instead of deliberately attacking the jurors.

### 3. Control Your Speed

You do not always need to hit the cars at maximum speed. Controlled collisions can help you avoid losing your vehicle too quickly.

### 4. Watch Your Vehicle's Condition

If your vehicle catches fire or becomes severely damaged, move away before it explodes. Find another vehicle and continue the objective.

### 5. Follow the Mission Markers

The mission markers make it easier to locate each juror. If you lose track of a target, check the map rather than driving around randomly.

## Common Mistakes in “Jury Fury”

### Killing the Jurors

The biggest mistake is treating the mission like a standard assassination objective. The goal is to scare the jurors, not eliminate them.

### Destroying Your Own Vehicle

Repeated high-speed collisions can destroy your vehicle before you finish the mission. Use controlled impacts and switch cars if necessary.

### Attracting Too Much Police Attention

Unnecessary chaos can make the mission harder. Try to complete the objectives quickly without creating additional trouble.

### Losing Track of the Target

The jurors can move when you begin attacking their vehicles. Keep an eye on the target and use the map to maintain your direction.

## Is “Jury Fury” Difficult?

“Jury Fury” is generally considered a straightforward early-game mission. The objectives are simple once you understand what the game expects.

The main challenge is knowing that vehicle damage is the primary method of intimidation. New players may assume they need to shoot the jurors, but that can make the mission unnecessarily difficult.

Once you focus on damaging the cars and keeping your own vehicle in good condition, the mission becomes much easier.

## What Happens After Completing “Jury Fury”?

After both jurors have been successfully intimidated, the mission ends and you receive your mission reward.

Completing “Jury Fury” also helps progress the early storyline involving Ken Rosenberg and Tommy's growing position in Vice City.

You can then continue with the next available missions and explore more of Vice City's story.

## Final Thoughts

The “Jury Fury” mission in GTA Vice City is mainly about understanding the objective rather than using complicated tactics. Tommy needs to intimidate two jurors, and damaging their vehicles is the key to completing the mission.

Use a reliable vehicle, follow the mission markers, avoid killing the targets, and keep an eye on your car's condition. Once you understand these mechanics, “Jury Fury” becomes a quick and manageable mission in GTA Vice City.
