If you are wondering what to do first in [GTA 6](/), prioritize completing Jason’s missable Chapter 1 preparation tasks before Lucia is released from prison, stash your early cash under the floorboards in Jason’s bedroom until you unlock a bank account, and buy a suppressor at Ammu-Nation. Once those early essentials are locked in, explore Key Lento, test the new Focus Mode door-scanning mechanic, and cruise the Leonida Keys to get a feel for the game's revamped driving and wanted systems.

Stepping into the state of Leonida for the first time can feel overwhelming. Between sprawling swamps, neon-lit Vice City districts, and deeper simulation mechanics than any previous Rockstar title, your opening hours set the tone for your entire playthrough. Use this prioritized checklist to avoid missing time-sensitive boosts while soaking in the best parts of the map.

## Quick-Start Priority Matrix (First 3 Hours)

| Priority Tier | Activity | Location / Trigger | Why It Matters? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Must-Do (Missable) | Complete Jason's prep tasks for Lucia | Chapter 1 (Just Like Paradise) | Disappears once Lucia joins you; grants an early relationship and campaign boost. |
| Survival & Cash | Stash cash under bedroom floorboards | Jason’s Safehouse | Protects your money from being lost before unlocking a bank account in Buck Me. |
| Stealth & Combat | Buy a suppressor | Any Ammu-Nation | Prevents instant wanted levels and keeps witness/CCTV heat low. |
| Exploration | Check dark blue buildings on the map | Statewide (Leonida) | Identifies enterable interiors vs. closed light-blue structures. |
| Traversal | Unlock RydeMe & transit routes | In-game smartphone | Acts as your primary fast-travel network across Leonida. |

## Finish Jason’s Missable Chapter 1 Prep Tasks

Before you get distracted by high-speed police chases, pay attention to the optional pre-release tasks in Chapter 1: Just Like Paradise. While Lucia is still serving time, Jason has a small window to prepare for her return. Fix up Jason’s house and his Vapid Ganado, get a fresh haircut, stock the fridge, pick up clothes for Lucia, and buy her a necklace and roses. These tasks permanently disappear once Lucia is released. Completing them gives the duo a stronger starting relationship dynamic and tangible early-game advantages.

## Hide Your Cash Under Jason’s Floorboards

You do not start GTA 6 with active banking access. Until you complete the mission Buck Me to open a proper bank account, carrying large sums of loose cash leaves you vulnerable. Head into Jason’s bedroom and tuck your extra bills under the loose floorboards. Treat it as your early-game vault every time you wrap up a side hustle.

## Grab a Suppressor at Ammu-Nation

Gunfire draws attention faster in Leonida than it did in Los Santos, especially with NPCs quick to pull out smartphones and report crimes. Make an early stop at Ammu-Nation to equip a suppressor on your primary sidearm. Pairing suppressed shots with stealthy movement lets you clear small-time smuggling jobs without triggering a multi-star police response.

## Test Focus Mode on Locked Doors

Rockstar introduced Focus Mode to help players read their environment before committing to a crime. Activate Focus Mode near storefronts, warehouses, and homes to see color-coded entry cues:

- **White doors:** Safe to enter or breach quietly.
- **Red doors:** Wired with alarms that will immediately alert local law enforcement.

## Learn the Map’s Color-Coded Building System

Instead of guessing which storefronts are just decorative facades, zoom in on your world map. Dark blue buildings indicate structures with accessible interiors or side entrances, while light blue buildings are closed off. Scanning dark blue blocks in new neighborhoods is the fastest way to find hidden loot, back-alley shortcuts, and interactive shops.

## Clear Out Key Lento’s Early Side Content

The opening chapter deliberately paces itself so you can settle into the southern reaches of the map. Before pushing north into the dense urban sprawl of Vice City, spend an hour clearing the odd jobs, encounters, and collectibles around Key Lento. The payouts give you enough breathing room to afford a dedicated garage once you leave the area.

## Save Up for Your First Garage

Vehicle persistence is much stricter in GTA 6, and leaving a customized ride abandoned on a highway shoulder is an easy way to lose it. As soon as you start branching out from Key Lento, make purchasing a garage your first major financial milestone so you can safely store and insure stolen or upgraded cars.

## Practice Losing the Cops Using the New Heat Factors

Outrunning law enforcement is no longer just about driving outside a flashing radar circle. GTA 6 tracks six distinct heat factors:

- Physical appearance
- Current outfit
- Visible weapons
- Vehicle model and plates
- Local CCTV footage
- Your partner’s status

Trigger a low-level wanted rating early just to practice breaking line of sight, swapping outfits in a back alley, holstering your weapons, and ditching a hot car.

## Set Up RydeMe for Fast Travel

There is no instant "click-anywhere-on-the-map" teleportation in GTA 6. Pull up your smartphone (Up on the D-pad) and familiarize yourself with RydeMe and local public transit routes. Once Lucia becomes playable alongside Jason, character switching also serves as a rapid way to jump between different parts of Leonida.

## Drive the Overseas Highway Through the Leonida Keys

Hop into a convertible or cruiser and take a sunset drive down the Leonida Keys. Beyond letting you test the heavier, more tactile vehicle suspension and tire grip physics, this tropical stretch shows off Rockstar's upgraded water shaders, coastal weather fronts, and roadside wildlife.

## Take an Airboat or Swamp Buggy into the Grassrivers

Head inland to the Grassrivers to experience the complete opposite of Vice City’s neon glamour. The mangrove swamps are packed with dynamic wildlife—including aggressive alligators—alongside off-the-grid smuggling caches and eerie rural mysteries. Just watch your step when wading through shallow water.

## Manage Your Character Weight and Fitness Early

GTA 6 brings back dynamic body composition. Living off gas station energy drinks, fast food, and multi-day benders visibly affects Jason and Lucia’s physique and stamina. Hit an outdoor beach gym or maintain a balanced diet early on so your sprint endurance doesn't tank during critical foot chases.

## Explore Ocean Beach and Little Cuba on Foot

When you finally roll into Vice City, park your car and walk a few blocks. Strolling past the pastel Art Deco hotels of Ocean Beach, grabbing a bite at a Little Cuba panadería, or browsing counterfeit goods at the Tisha Wocka flea market lets you absorb dense ambient NPC routines and overheard conversations you would miss at 100 mph.

## Check Out the Underground Scene in Port Gellhorn

If you want gritty, old-school GTA chaos, ride a dirt bike out to Port Gellhorn. Once a booming tourist destination, this faded resort town runs on cheap motels, biker turf, and back-alley hustles—making it one of the best places to find early-game street races and impromptu brawls.

## Keep Your Criminal Profile Balanced

Every major choice and public rampage feeds into Jason and Lucia’s Criminal Profile. If you push either protagonist all the way to an Irredeemable rating, that status is permanent and puts severe strain on their partnership. Save your game manually before going on an unprovoked city-wide rampage so you can reload a clean slate afterward.

## Final Thoughts

Mastering your opening hours in GTA 6 comes down to smart preparation before chaos takes over. By knocking out Jason’s missable Chapter 1 tasks, stashing early cash under the floorboards, and learning Leonida’s new heat and Focus Mode mechanics, you set Jason and Lucia up for long-term success. Take your time exploring Vice City’s distinct districts, keep your criminal profile balanced, and enjoy everything Leonida has to offer.
