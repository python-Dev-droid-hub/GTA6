The “Sir, Yes Sir!” mission in GTA Vice City is one of the more exciting missions involving the military. Tommy Vercetti is asked to steal a Rhino tank from an army convoy and deliver it safely before the soldiers can stop him. The mission requires good timing, careful driving, and a quick escape.

If you are wondering how to steal the tank in GTA Vice City, this blog explains the complete process. You will learn where to start the mission, how to stop the military convoy, how to get inside the Rhino tank, and where to take it after the theft.

## How to Start the “Sir, Yes Sir!” Mission?

The “Sir, Yes Sir!” mission is given by Phil Cassidy at his location in Little Haiti. After completing the required previous missions, you can visit Phil to begin the mission.

When the mission starts, Phil explains that he wants Tommy to obtain a military tank. The tank is traveling through Vice City as part of an army convoy.

Your objective is straightforward:

- Find the military convoy.
- Stop the Rhino tank.
- Get inside the tank.
- Escape from the soldiers.
- Deliver the Rhino to the required location.

The difficult part is getting the tank without being overwhelmed by the military.

## Where to Find the Military Convoy?

After the mission begins, follow the mission marker on your map. It leads you toward the military convoy carrying the Rhino tank.

The convoy consists of military vehicles and armed soldiers. The Rhino is heavily protected, so approaching it directly can quickly turn into a fight.

Before reaching the convoy, make sure Tommy has enough health and armor. Having a suitable vehicle can also make the approach easier.

You do not need to destroy every military vehicle. Your main goal is to get the Rhino tank and escape.

## How to Stop the Tank?

Once you reach the convoy, position your vehicle near the Rhino. The convoy will continue moving, so you need to create an opportunity to reach the tank.

One useful strategy is to drive ahead of the convoy and slow down or stop near the Rhino's path. This can force the convoy to slow down and gives Tommy a chance to reach the tank.

Be careful because the soldiers will immediately react when you interfere with the convoy.

Avoid wasting time fighting every soldier. The longer you remain near the convoy, the more dangerous the situation becomes.

## How to Steal the Rhino Tank?

Getting inside the Rhino is the most important part of the mission.

When you reach the tank, move close to its entrance and enter it like a normal vehicle. Once Tommy gets inside, the Rhino becomes your vehicle.

The military will immediately try to stop you.

At this point, do not stay around to fight. Start driving away from the convoy as quickly as possible.

The Rhino is extremely durable, so it can withstand significant damage. However, that does not mean you should ignore the pursuing soldiers.

## Escape From the Military

After stealing the tank, your wanted level increases and the military begins chasing Tommy.

This is where many players make a simple mistake: they stop the Rhino and fight the soldiers.

Instead, keep moving.

Use the Rhino's strength to push through traffic and military vehicles. Roads with fewer obstacles can make your escape easier. Avoid getting trapped between buildings, walls, or other vehicles.

The tank is powerful, but it is not invincible. Continuous attacks can eventually cause serious damage.

Your goal is not to defeat the entire military force. Your goal is to get the Rhino to the destination.

## Where to Take the Tank?

Once you successfully steal the Rhino, follow the mission marker to the delivery location.

Keep checking the radar because the destination is more important than engaging enemies. If soldiers are following you, continue driving toward the marker instead of stopping to fight.

The Rhino's size can make some streets difficult to navigate. Take wider roads whenever possible and avoid tight areas where the tank can become stuck.

When you reach the marked destination, position the Rhino correctly and complete the delivery.

## Tips for Completing “Sir, Yes Sir!”

### 1. Get Armor Before Starting

Armor provides extra protection against gunfire and explosions. It can make the initial approach to the military convoy much easier.

### 2. Do Not Fight Everyone

You do not need to eliminate every soldier. Focus on stealing the Rhino and getting away.

### 3. Keep the Tank Moving

A moving Rhino is harder to surround. Once you have stolen it, drive toward the objective instead of stopping unnecessarily.

### 4. Use the Radar

The radar helps you track the mission destination and identify nearby enemies. Keep an eye on it while escaping.

### 5. Avoid Narrow Streets

The Rhino is large and can become difficult to control in tight areas. Wider roads provide more room for turning and escaping.

### 6. Do Not Waste Time After Stealing the Tank

The longer you remain near the convoy, the more enemies can surround you. Get inside the Rhino and leave the area quickly.

## What Makes the Mission Difficult?

The biggest challenge in “Sir, Yes Sir!” is not driving the Rhino. It is taking the tank while dealing with the military.

The convoy is protected, and the soldiers react immediately when Tommy attempts to steal the vehicle. This means players who approach without a plan can lose health quickly.

Another challenge is escaping after the theft. The military continues to pursue Tommy, so simply getting inside the Rhino does not finish the mission.

The safest approach is to treat the mission as a quick theft rather than a long battle.

## Can You Destroy the Military Convoy?

You can cause significant destruction while driving the Rhino, but destroying every military vehicle is unnecessary for completing the mission.

The Rhino itself has powerful weapons and can easily push through ordinary traffic and vehicles. However, spending too much time attacking the military can create additional problems.

The mission objective remains the same: steal the Rhino and deliver it to the required destination.

## Common Mistakes Players Make

Some players make the mission harder than it needs to be by trying to fight the entire convoy.

Another common mistake is approaching the tank too slowly. The convoy can continue moving, making it harder to reach the Rhino.

Players can also get distracted after stealing the tank. Once the Rhino is under your control, the best option is usually to follow the mission marker and keep moving.

Finally, avoid getting the Rhino stuck against buildings or obstacles. Its large size makes recovery difficult when it is trapped in a narrow space.

## “Sir, Yes Sir!” Mission Walkthrough in Short

If you want a quick version of the mission, follow these steps:

1. Go to Phil Cassidy's location to start the mission.
2. Follow the mission marker toward the military convoy.
3. Locate the Rhino tank.
4. Approach the tank carefully.
5. Enter the Rhino as soon as you get the opportunity.
6. Drive away from the military convoy.
7. Avoid unnecessary fights during the escape.
8. Follow the radar and mission marker.
9. Deliver the Rhino to the marked destination.
10. Complete the mission.

## Final Thoughts

The “Sir, Yes Sir!” mission is memorable because it gives Tommy Vercetti the chance to steal one of the most powerful vehicles in GTA Vice City. The Rhino tank makes the escape much easier once you get inside, but reaching it safely is the real challenge.

The key is to act quickly. Approach the convoy, take the Rhino, avoid unnecessary combat, and drive directly toward the destination. With the right timing and a little patience, you can complete “Sir, Yes Sir!” without too much trouble.

Once the mission is finished, Tommy is one step closer to taking full control of Vice City.
