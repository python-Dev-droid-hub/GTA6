Yes, you can customize cars in [GTA 6](/). Launch gameplay documents paint previews, liveries, engine swaps, and full build kits for Jason’s Vapid Ganado. Whether GTA 6 has more upgrades than GTA 5 is harder to answer. The clearest examples show what you can do with one pickup, while a full comparison would need to cover several vehicles and shops in both games.

The Ganado is a useful starting point because it shows three choices you can make in the workshop. You can change the truck’s look, swap its engine, or select a full build that bundles several changes together.

## What Can You Customize in GTA 6?

You can customize Jason’s Vapid Ganado with new paint finishes, liveries, engine swaps, and full build kits. At Coastal Auto Care, preview paint colors on the truck and compare how engine options affect speed, acceleration, and handling. Full builds bundle several upgrades, including paint, interior changes, and select engine modifications.

### Paint and Liveries

At Coastal Auto Care, the Cosmetics → Respray menu lets you preview paint on the Ganado before paying. Launch footage shows gloss and solid metallic finishes, color filters, and prices that can change with the finish. If you like a particular shade, check it in both finishes before you choose.

A respray changes the vehicle’s paint color. A livery adds a design, such as stripes or other graphics. TotalGTA’s workshop coverage documents multiple liveries for the Ganado, so you can give the same pickup a plain finish or a more distinctive look.

### Engine Swaps and Full Builds

TotalGTA documents engine swaps for the Ganado at Coastal Auto Care. The workshop’s stat panel shows how a selection affects figures such as speed, acceleration, paved-road handling, and off-road handling. If you’re building a truck for dirt roads, the off-road handling figure gives you something specific to compare before buying.

The Pro Builds / Full Kits menu offers another route. One documented kit bundles a custom paint job, interior changes, and engine modifications. That gives you a ready-made build if you want the whole truck to look and drive differently in one purchase.

## Where Are the GTA 6 Mod Shops?

Open the map’s location index and look under Vehicles → Mod Shops. The launch guides identify these three locations, each with a different reason to visit.

| Mod Shop | Location | What to Look For? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Coastal Auto Care | Key Lento | Paint, liveries, and engine options documented for Jason’s Ganado. This shop also applies the Ultimate Edition Ganado Retro Build. |
| Rideout Customs | Crosstown, Vice City | An Ultimate Edition shop focused on show-car styling, including distinctive rims, custom interiors, and donk builds. |
| One-Eyed Willie's | Lake Leonida area | An Ultimate Edition shop specializing in off-road customization and hand-painted designs. |

## Does GTA 6 Have More Car Upgrades Than GTA 5?

GTA 6 has documented engine swaps, bundled builds, and shops with distinct specialties. A larger overall upgrade catalog remains unverified. GTA 5 story mode already offers a broad set of options at Los Santos Customs, including resprays, wheels, bodywork, engine upgrades, brakes, suspension, transmissions, and turbo tuning.

| Upgrade Category | GTA 6 Launch Evidence | GTA 5 Story Mode |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Paint and Styling | The Ganado has documented resprays, finish previews, and liveries. | Los Santos Customs offers resprays, wheels, and bodywork. |
| Engine Upgrades | Coastal Auto Care shows multiple engine choices for the Ganado. | Los Santos Customs offers staged engine upgrades and turbo tuning. |
| Brakes, Suspension, and Transmission | The Ganado examples leave the full range of parts unverified. | Los Santos Customs offers all three upgrade categories. |
| Workshop Specialties | Documented shops cover retro, show-car, and off-road styling. | Los Santos Customs groups visual and performance upgrades in one workshop menu. |

If you care most about how a car drives, compare the available parts and their effects on the vehicle you plan to keep. If you care most about how it looks, the shop specialties and full kits give you a better sense of what GTA 6 offers than a count of menu categories.

## Conclusion

GTA 6 vehicle customization gives players paint previews, liveries, engine swaps, and full builds. Whether it offers more upgrades than GTA 5 remains unverified across vehicles and editions. Start at Coastal Auto Care with Jason’s Ganado, compare the available parts and their effects, and spend your cash on upgrades you want.
