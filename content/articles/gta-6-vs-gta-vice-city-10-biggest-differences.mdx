Grand Theft Auto VI brings players back to Vice City as Lucia Caminos and Jason Duval, with the city now part of the fictional state of Leonida. The original Grand Theft Auto: Vice City follows Tommy Vercetti as he builds a criminal empire in 1986. You’ll recognize the pastel hotels and waterfront, but the two games give you different reasons to explore what’s beyond the beach.

Rockstar released [GTA 6](/) on November 19, 2026, for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. This comparison uses the 2002 original as its reference point for GTA Vice City.

| Feature | GTA 6 | GTA Vice City |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Setting | Modern-day Vice City and the state of Leonida | Vice City in 1986 |
| Playable leads | Lucia Caminos and Jason Duval | Tommy Vercetti |
| Story premise | A failed score pulls a couple into a statewide conspiracy | An ambushed deal sets Tommy on a path to take over the city |
| Map focus | City streets, island communities, wetlands, and rural areas | A city-centered map built around islands and bridges |
| Original launch hardware | PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X\|S | PlayStation 2 |

## The Setting Moves from 1986 to Modern-Day Vice City

The original Vice City treats 1986 as part of its identity. The pastel clothes, radio lineup, and extravagant nightlife give Tommy’s rise the feel of a period crime story. Even a short drive puts you back in that era because the music and presentation work together.

GTA 6 uses that same coastal setting to portray modern life. Rockstar’s character profiles feature internet culture and a local music industry alongside the clubs and beaches. The familiar city is a backdrop for a different generation of characters and ambitions.

## Leonida Takes the Map Beyond the City

In the original, you spend most of your time learning the city’s neighborhoods, bridges, and islands. Familiar routes become part of how you play. Once you know the city, a drive between missions can feel like navigating a place you’ve lived in.

GTA 6 builds its world around several distinct regions. Beyond Vice City, there are the Leonida Keys, Grassrivers wetlands, Port Gellhorn, Ambrosia, and Mount Kalaga National Park. The practical difference is the variety of routes and landscapes. A trip out of town can take you from city traffic to island roads or into the countryside.

## Some Familiar Landmarks Return Alongside New Neighborhoods

Ocean Beach is an easy place to see what carries over. Its beachfront and pastel Art Deco hotels are recognizable in both games. GTA 6 also features places such as Little Cuba, the Tisha-Wocka flea market, and VC Port. The Cuban neighborhood in the original is called Little Havana.

If you remember the original map by heart, Ocean Beach gives you a familiar starting point. From there, the new districts give you a reason to learn the city again instead of relying on the routes you used as Tommy.

## Two Playable Leads Change Whose Story You Experience

The original keeps the focus on Tommy Vercetti, a mob associate sent south by Sonny Forelli after serving time in prison. Every alliance and setback feeds into Tommy’s rise.

GTA 6 follows Lucia Caminos, newly out of prison, and Jason Duval, a former soldier who works for drug runners in the Keys. Their different backgrounds give each a stake in the story, and their relationship connects those perspectives.

## The Story Shifts from Building an Empire to Surviving Together

Both games begin with a criminal deal going wrong, but what happens next gives each campaign its direction. Tommy loses money and merchandise in an ambush. As pressure mounts from his old boss, he sets out to establish power of his own. GTA 6’s failed score draws Jason and Lucia into a conspiracy that stretches across Leonida and forces them to rely on each other.

That difference shows up in the quieter scenes. Vice City measures Tommy’s progress through the territory and businesses he gains. In GTA 6, even time spent at home can show what Jason and Lucia have built together and what they stand to lose.

## Property Ownership Gives the Original Its Own Progression

Buying a business in GTA Vice City can unlock a set of missions and a steady source of cash. The Print Works and Malibu Club are examples of assets that give you new jobs to do. After you finish a business’s required missions, it generates income you can collect. That loop makes buying property a tangible part of Tommy’s rise.

GTA 6 gives some of its quieter playable moments to Jason and Lucia’s shared life. Ars Technica describes assembling furniture together and seeing the game register a change in their relationship. Both games use the spaces characters spend time in to show progress. For Tommy, a business brings in money. For Jason and Lucia, an ordinary moment at home can strengthen their bond.

## Side Activities Change the Pace Between Missions

The original’s taxi fares, vigilante missions, and rampages suit short bursts of play. You can make progress on a side activity, then get back to the story. Those optional jobs fit a game where learning the fastest route or beating a timer is part of the fun.

GTA 6 also gives you slower ways to spend time. Reviewers describe mini golf, gym workouts, and time at the beach alongside the chases and robberies. These activities give you a reason to linger in a neighborhood and see how the characters live between major story events.

## GTA 6 Shows More of Everyday Life at Street Level

The original establishes a neighborhood’s identity quickly through its buildings, traffic, and radio. The beach strip, business districts, and residential streets are easy to recognize as you move between them.

GTA 6 gives those places more everyday detail. Reviewers describe going shopping, helping a neighbor with a car, and spending time with friends by the water. That attention to ordinary routines changes what you notice when you slow down. A place can be worth visiting for what happens there between missions.

## The Radio Reflects a Different Era of Music and Celebrity

The 2002 game uses period music to establish its time and place. Salsa and 1980s pop show up early, and changing stations gives the same drive a different mood. The radio does much of the work of making the city feel like 1986.

GTA 6 uses music and radio to build a contemporary media scene. Its lineup features names such as Bad Bunny and Burna Boy, while Charlamagne tha God and Ivy Rivera host the in-game program Early AF. Rockstar also publishes a separate guide to the score, soundtrack, and selected stations.

## The Two Games Launched on Different Console Generations

GTA Vice City debuted on PlayStation 2 in 2002 and later reached other platforms. The Definitive Edition is a later release of Tommy’s game. When comparing the games’ presentation, check which version of Vice City you’re looking at, because screenshots from the remaster and the original represent different releases.

GTA 6 launched worldwide on November 19, 2026, for PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. Rockstar’s launch announcement lists those two console families.

## Final Thoughts

GTA 6 and GTA Vice City share a setting but offer different experiences. The original follows Tommy Vercetti as he builds a criminal empire in 1986. GTA 6 follows Lucia and Jason across modern-day Leonida. Play the classic for its eighties atmosphere, or choose GTA 6 for its broader world.
