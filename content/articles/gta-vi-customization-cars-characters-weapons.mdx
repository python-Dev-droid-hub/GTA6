[GTA VI](/) customization transforms how you interact with Vice City and the wider state of Leonida by tying visual upgrades directly to survival, stealth, and police evasion. Rather than serving as purely cosmetic fluff, changing Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos’ outfits, swapping license plates or paint jobs at Pay 'n' Spray, and stowing custom firearms in your personal vehicle’s trunk actively alter how law enforcement tracks you and how you approach every heist.

## Car Customization: Specialized Mod Shops, Fuel, and Trunk Storage

Vehicle customization in Grand Theft Auto VI spans 14 specialized mod shops, 19 Pay 'n' Spray locations, and 6 car washes scattered across Leonida's counties—including Vice-Dale, Mariana, Kelly, Leonard, and Lummox. Instead of every garage offering identical parts, workshops feature distinct specialties such as retro restorations (Coastal Auto Care), muscle builds (Capri Classic), off-road tuning in Kelly, livery design in Vice-Dale, and motorcycle upgrades in Lummox.

### Key Vehicle Systems to Know

Register stolen rides at Pay 'n' Spray or Moses's garage to keep them permanently, outrun advanced anti-theft trackers, respray paint to shake police recognition, and refuel at gas stations.

### Registration & Security Tiers

Stolen vehicles cannot simply be parked in a garage to claim ownership. You must pay a registration fee via Pay 'n' Spray (tracked through the in-game WAiNK phone app) to make a stolen car permanent. High-end supercars also feature advanced anti-theft trackers that require a key cloner rather than a basic slim jim.

### Tactical Resprays

Police remember both your vehicle model and its color after a crime. Visiting one of the 19 Pay 'n' Spray shops changes your base coat so officers no longer recognize the car on sight.

### Fuel & Maintenance

Every vehicle tracks fuel/battery levels and engine condition. Pulling into a gas station tops off your tank and repairs minor body damage, which also preserves the resale value of boosted cars.

### Exclusive Edition Garages

Ultimate Edition players unlock two additional custom shops—Rideout Customs in Crosstown and One-Eyed Willie's in Lake Leonida—alongside the Ganado Retro Build upgrade for Jason's signature Vapid Ganado.

## Character Customization: Outfits, Salons, Tattoos, and Fitness

While Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos have fixed facial structures and backstories rather than a blank-slate creator, Rockstar gives you granular control over their physical build, grooming, and wardrobe.

### Outfits as Police Disguises

Clothing stores across Leonida sell full preset ensembles as well as individual tops, bottoms, footwear, watches, eyewear, and hats. You can save up to 10 custom outfits in your safehouse wardrobe and store up to 5 outfits (plus 3 of each accessory type) inside your personal car's trunk. Because witnesses and police identify you by the clothes you wore during a felony, swapping outfits out of sight actively strips away police recognition—though changing a hat or sunglasses alone will not fool law enforcement.

### Hair, Facial Hair, Makeup, and Nails

Grooming is split between your safehouse mirror and commercial stylists:

- **Safehouse Mirror:** Strictly reductive—you can trim Jason's hair shorter or shave his head and beard at home, but you cannot add new styles.
- **Salons & Barbers:** Shops like Barnacle Beauties and regional barbers sell signature haircuts and dyes. Ultimate Edition owners also gain access to Sara's Unisex Salon in Hamlet, adding exclusive hairstyles, beard trims for Jason, and customizable makeup and nail options for Lucia.

### Tattoos and Body Composition

Across Leonida, 11 tattoo studios (marked by black heart icons) offer regional ink for both protagonists, supplemented by over 50 exclusive designs by the art collective FAILE at Stockyard's Electric Fang Tattoo Parlour in the Ultimate Edition. Meanwhile, the pause menu tracks Jason and Lucia’s height, weight, calories, sleep, muscle mass, body fat, and strength separately. Hitting local gyms increases muscularity and melee strength, whereas overeating adds body weight.

## Weapon Customization: Ammu-Nation Mods and Loadout Limits

GTA VI replaces the bottomless weapon wheel of older entries with a grounded, tactical carry system. At any moment, Jason or Lucia can carry two concealed sidearms, two visible longarms, explosives, one melee weapon, and utility gear like binoculars or a flashlight. Openly brandishing a rifle or shotgun on the street alerts civilians and nearby cops, forcing you to holster sidearms or stow longarms inside your car's trunk.

### Attachments and Independent Protagonist Builds

Buying a firearm at Ammu-Nation unlocks that base weapon for both Jason and Lucia simultaneously, yet each protagonist customizes and equips their own version independently. For example, Jason can outfit a Duke 556 assault rifle with heavy combat barrels and extended magazines while Lucia configures the exact same rifle with a suppressor and low-profile optic for stealth.

### Ammu-Nation Workbench Upgrades

Grips, magazines, optics, muzzle brakes, barrels, and cosmetic wraps must be installed at an Ammu-Nation shop. Buying more inventory builds loyalty with store owners, unlocking higher stock tiers and rare finishes.

### On-the-Fly Field Toggles

Suppressors and tactical flashlights can be attached or detached directly from your loadout wheel during free-roam exploration without visiting a gun store.

### 120+ Unique Weapon Variants

Marked by gold icons in the world and weapon lockers, these bespoke firearms (such as signature pistols and custom shotguns) feature fixed stats and custom finishes that cannot be modified.

## Beyond Gear: Safehouses, Stash Bags, and Mobile Inventories

Customization extends into how you prepare for jobs across Leonida. Your personal vehicle's trunk functions as a mobile base—retaining stored weapons, ammo packs, body armor, and spare outfits even if you are wasted or busted (unlike unowned stolen cars, where stored gear is lost). You can also organize contraband and heist loot using stash bags, dropping them in your trunk before a getaway or tracking lost bags on the map if a shootout goes sideways.

GTA VI customization elevates player agency by blending deep personal expression with high-stakes survival mechanics. Whether tuning a ride across Leonida’s specialized mod shops, assembling bespoke weapon loadouts, or swapping outfits to evade police heat, every cosmetic choice directly shapes how Jason and Lucia conquer Vice City’s criminal underworld.
