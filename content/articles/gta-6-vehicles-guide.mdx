Vehicles have always been the beating heart of Rockstar Games’ open-world sandbox, and Grand Theft Auto VI is taking that philosophy to unprecedented levels. Set across the sprawling expanses of Leonida and the neon-drenched streets of Vice City, exploring the map with protagonists Jason and Lucia will require mastering an immense roster of transportation.

From returning fan favorites to jaw-dropping real-world-inspired newcomers, here is your complete guide to what to expect from the cars, bikes, boats, and aircraft of GTA 6.

## The Evolution of Driving in Leonida

Rockstar is introducing a massive garage of vehicles spanning nearly 20 distinct categories. Leaks, official trailers, and data breakdowns point to a vehicle count scaling past the 180–200 mark, balancing classic Grand Theft Auto satire with deeply authentic automotive culture. Physics have also received an overhaul, offering a tighter distinction between the heavy slide of classic muscle cars and the downforce-heavy grip of modern supercars.

## Cars: From Everyday Sedans to Exotic Supercars

Whether you are running from a five-star police response or cruising down ocean-side highways, the car lineup is broader than ever. Vehicles are split neatly into familiar classes packed with deep cuts:

### Super & Sports Cars

High-end performance makes a heavy statement in Vice City. Expect returning powerhouses alongside new additions heavily inspired by real-world exotics, such as the Grotti Furia (Ferrari 488 Pista/SF90), Pegassi Tempesta (Lamborghini Huracán), and sleek new Audi and Porsche-inspired sports coupes and convertibles.

### Muscle Cars

Perfect for burning rubber down strip malls or coastal boulevards. Classics like the Albany Buccaneer and Bravado Gauntlet series are joined by aggressive new interpretations of modern and vintage American muscle.

### SUVs & Trucks

Given Leonida’s mix of dense urban centers, swamps, and rural outback, utility vehicles are essential. Heavy-hitters like the Vapid Aleutian (Ford Expedition style) and various lifted pickups make navigating rough terrain a breeze.

## Bikes and Two-Wheelers

For players who prefer weaving through dense gridlock or tearing up dirt trails, the two-wheeled selection features incredible variety:

### Sports Bikes & Superbikes

High-performance street bikes—like the Ducati-inspired Principe Alvino V1 and the returning Bati 801—offer blistering acceleration, making them ideal for narrow escapes.

### Choppers & Dirt Bikes

Cruisers like the LCC Avarus fit right into the southern biker aesthetic, while dirt bikes like the Maibatsu Sanchez are built for mud-soaked shortcuts through the backwoods.

### Bicycles

Classic BMX and mountain bikes return for low-profile, neighborhood cruising.

## Boats and Watercraft: Conquering Vice City’s Coastlines

Water plays a much larger role in GTA 6 than in past titles, featuring expansive ocean views, winding swamp bayous, and navigable rivers. The maritime lineup reflects this aquatic focus:

### Speedboats and Yachts

Luxury liners like the Azimut 85-inspired yacht sit alongside high-speed powerboats like the Shitzu Squalo and cigarette boats designed for open-water getaways.

### Airboats and Kayaks

Essential for cutting through the shallow, marshy wetlands of the Leonida Everglades. Airboats equipped with massive rear fans and even leisure craft like kayaks add layers of immersion to rural exploration.

### Personal Watercraft

Jet skis like the Seashark return for fast-paced beach-hopping.

## Aircraft: Taking to the Skies

When the heat gets too intense on the ground, escaping upward is your best bet. While detailed aircraft lists are still emerging, players can expect a mix of reliable rotary and fixed-wing transport:

### Helicopters

Trusted law enforcement and civilian choppers like the Maverick, Police Maverick, and the combat-ready Buzzard Attack Chopper are confirmed to make their return.

### Planes

From crop dusters (Duster) for low-altitude scenic flights to luxury private jets (Buckingham Shamal / Nimbus) for cross-map transit, air travel remains seamless.

## Final Thoughts

Whether you are carving up the neon streets of Vice City in a hyper-tuned supercar, tearing through the Leonida swamplands on an airboat, or fleeing the law on a sport bike, GTA 6 promises a breathtaking fleet of vehicles. Mastering this massive automotive ecosystem will be key to conquering the map. Start planning your ultimate garage now, because when Jason and Lucia hit the road, every ride matters.
