No, [Grand Theft Auto VI](/) does not include a mission replay menu at launch. Once you complete a main story mission in Leonida, you cannot select it from the pause menu to play it again. Aside from one post-game exception for the final mission, the only way to revisit earlier missions is by loading a manual save file created before you started the job.

This marks a major shift from GTA 5 and Red Dead Redemption 2, both of which let players replay missions to earn gold medals or test different approaches. If you want to experience every branch of Jason and Lucia's story without starting a fresh campaign from scratch, you need to plan your save slots carefully.

## Why Did Rockstar Cut Mission Replay in GTA 6?

Rockstar Games intentionally left out the mission replay feature to preserve narrative weight and player consequence.

Speaking to Kotaku, Rockstar Senior Design Director Scott Butchard confirmed that the team considered adding mission replay early in development before scrapping it. According to Butchard, stepping outside the timeline to replay missions felt "alien and disconnected" from the narrative and diminished the impact of the choices players made. Because GTA 6 tracks your decisions, criminal reputation, and shifting chapter outcomes, locking in your actions keeps the stakes high across the entire campaign.

## The One Exception: Replaying the Chapter 5 Finale

While Chapters 1 through 4 are one-and-done, you can replay the final mission of Chapter 5.

After the end credits roll, GTA 6 places you back into post-game free roam on the Leonida map at a point just before the finale begins. The mission marker stays active on your map, allowing you to launch the finale whenever you want. This lets you explore the open world freely while still giving you the option to test different choices in the final sequence.

## How to Replay Missions Using Manual Save Slots?

Because there is no built-in mission select menu, manual saves are your best workaround. GTA 6 gives you 15 manual save slots, which is plenty of room to create permanent checkpoints before major turning points.

To create a manual save at any time outside of an active mission:

1. Open the Pause Menu.
2. Navigate to Game > Save Game.
3. Select an empty slot and confirm.

### Best Moments to Create Backup Saves

Use your 15 slots strategically before major chapter cutoffs and branching decisions so you never lose hours of progress:

| Campaign Stage | Recommended Save Timing | Why This Checkpoint Matters? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Chapter 1 | Before starting Tourist Trap (Take Up Mattia's Offer) | Accepting Mattia's job triggers a locked sequence that pushes you straight into Chapter 2. |
| Chapter 2 | Before Checking Out (Move to Vice City) | Protects your chance to finish early Vice City errands and time-sensitive encounters. |
| Chapter 3 | Before key heist setups and Halifax's storyline | Lets you test both outcomes of Halifax's choice and claim unique weapons and vehicles. |
| Chapter 4 | Before Power Play and before Starting From Zero | Power Play is the cutoff for Felipe's optional mission and reward vehicle. |
| Chapter 5 | Before Protective Custody and The End of the Road | Allows you to revisit late-game decisions that directly shape your ending. |

Chapter transitions also reshuffle safehouses and parked rides—plan storage with [GTA 6 Safehouse Management](/news/gta-6-safehouse-management).

## Do You Need Mission Replay for 100% Completion?

No. You do not need to replay story missions to reach 100% completion in GTA 6. Unlike GTA 5, there is no gold medal checklist tied to replaying campaign levels. Once you finish the story, you can complete remaining strangers, collectibles, and open-world activities during post-game free roam.

However, certain side encounters marked with an hourglass icon and unique mid-mission weapons can expire if you advance the story too fast. Prioritize hourglass markers as soon as they appear on your map so you do not have to roll back to an older save.

## Final Thoughts

Even without a traditional mission replay menu, planning ahead makes experiencing everything in GTA 6 simple. By rotating your 15 manual save slots before major chapter transitions and prioritizing time-sensitive hourglass encounters, you can explore every branching choice without replaying the entire campaign. And once the credits roll, post-game free roam lets you revisit the Chapter 5 finale or wrap up remaining open-world activities across Leonida at your own pace.
