The [GTA 6](/) Wanted Level system completely overhauls how law enforcement tracks, pursues, and identifies players across the state of Leonida. Gone are the days when driving down a highway at top speed and hiding in an alley for thirty seconds guaranteed a clean getaway. Rockstar Games has introduced a dual-layered law enforcement model that pairs traditional Wanted Stars with a persistent Heat and Identification system.

Whether you are pulling off a high-stakes robbery in Vice City or accidentally triggering a pursuit in the rural stretches of Kelly County, understanding how police gather evidence and coordinate their searches is essential for survival.

## Wanted Stars vs. The Heat System: How Law Enforcement Tracks You?

In Grand Theft Auto VI, law enforcement relies on two distinct mechanics working in tandem: Wanted Levels (response intensity) and Heat (suspect identification).

### Wanted Stars (Response Intensity)

Returning with a maximum cap of six stars, your Wanted Level dictates the severity of the police response. However, the color and state of the stars communicate vital tactical information:

- **Gray/Outlined Stars:** A crime has been reported by a witness or alarm, and officers are en route to investigate, though they do not yet have a visual on you.
- **Solid White Stars:** Police have direct line of sight, initiating an active pursuit.
- **Solid Red Stars:** Officers have lost visual contact, shifting the pursuit into an active search phase where patrols sweep the surrounding red-shaded perimeter on your map.

### Heat and Clue Icons (Suspect Identification)

Beneath your Wanted Stars sits a row of Heat icons. These indicators reveal exactly what identifying details witnesses, officers, or surveillance cameras have logged into the police BOLO (Be On the Lookout) broadcast:

- **Face/Appearance:** Witnesses or cops saw your unmasked face or distinct hairstyle.
- **Outfit:** Law enforcement has a description of your current clothing.
- **Vehicle:** Your car's make, color, or license plate is flagged.
- **Weapon:** Officers know the specific firearm class you used.
- **Accomplice:** Police know whether Jason and Lucia committed the crime together.
- **CCTV Footage:** Local or cloud-backed security cameras recorded the incident.

## Smarter Police AI Strategies in Leonida

Rockstar has significantly upgraded enemy awareness to eliminate predictable AI exploits:

### No Omniscient Minimap Radar

Police do not automatically appear as omniscient cones on your minimap. You must rely on visual sightlines, audio cues, and the unlocked police scanner to hear dispatch callouts about your suspected direction and helicopter fuel status.

### 911 Witness Reporting

Crimes aren't instantly telepathic. Civilians pull out smartphones to call 911. Stopping or intimidating witnesses before they finish the call can prevent a Wanted Level from triggering altogether.

### K9 Search Units

Hiding indefinitely in a single bush or dumpster no longer guarantees safety. If you linger too long inside an active search zone, police deploy tracking dogs to flush you out.

### Escalation Sensitivity

How you fight back matters. Shooting out police vehicle tires or aiming at non-lethal yellow target zones in focus mode escalates your Wanted Level far slower than executing officers.

## Proven Escape Tactics: How to Lose the Cops in GTA 6

Escaping law enforcement in GTA 6 requires dismantling their information trail step by step rather than relying purely on horsepower.

### Break Line of Sight and Go Off-Road

Straightaway speed rarely works at three stars or higher because heavy Vice City traffic and highway spike strips trap fast cars. Make sharp, unpredictable turns into narrow alleys, cut through construction sites, or abandon your vehicle to slip through pedestrian walkways and fences.

### Clear Your Heat Clues Systematically

Once the stars turn red (indicating cops have lost sight of you), immediately erase the clues listed under your Wanted bar:

#### Ditch the Vehicle

Abandon a flagged car out of sight and steal a clean vehicle—or visit a mod garage to swap license plates and apply a fresh coat of paint.

#### Swap Your Outfit

Store spare clothes in your personal vehicle's trunk. Changing your jacket, pants, or accessories clears the Outfit and Appearance icons. (Always wear a mask before starting a planned robbery to prevent facial identification.)

#### Split Up Jason and Lucia

If witnesses report a male and female duo working together, the Accomplice icon activates. When playing in free roam outside of locked mission scripts, separating Jason and Lucia—having them flee in different directions or vehicles—breaks the suspect profile police are searching for.

#### Destroy Local CCTV Evidence

Before fleeing a store robbery or restricted facility, look for security terminals. Destroying local CCTV servers erases video evidence before dispatch can log your face and outfit into the BOLO system.

### Use Terrain and Scanner Windows Against Helicopters

Police choppers are the biggest obstacle to breaking line of sight. Duck under highway overpasses, inside underground parking garages, or beneath dense Everglades mangrove canopies to block aerial thermal and visual tracking. Alternatively, listen to your police scanner—dispatch will announce when a helicopter is low on fuel and returning to base, giving you a critical window to vanish.

## Final Thoughts

Mastering the GTA 6 Wanted Level system means outsmarting law enforcement rather than simply outdriving them. By breaking line of sight, destroying CCTV evidence, and clearing your active Heat clues—from ditching flagged vehicles to swapping outfits—you can dismantle police BOLO profiles and slip away cleanly. Keep spare gear in your trunk, listen closely to your police scanner, and stay one step ahead across Leonida.
