The “Keep Your Friends Close...” mission is the final story mission in GTA Vice City. It brings Tommy Vercetti’s criminal empire to a dramatic conclusion as Sonny Forelli arrives in Vice City to collect his money. What begins as a tense confrontation quickly turns into a massive shootout at the Vercetti Estate.

Completing this mission requires good weapon preparation, quick movement, and careful defense of your mansion. In this blog, you’ll learn how to start Keep Your Friends Close..., what happens during the mission, how to protect your money, and how to defeat Sonny and his gang.

## How to Start the “Keep Your Friends Close...” Mission?

Before you can access the final mission, you need to complete the required story and asset missions in GTA Vice City. Once the necessary conditions are met, the “Keep Your Friends Close...” mission becomes available at the Vercetti Estate.

Go to the Vercetti Estate and enter the mission marker to trigger the final confrontation. Tommy discovers that Sonny Forelli has arrived in Vice City and wants the money he believes Tommy owes him.

The situation quickly escalates, forcing Tommy to defend his mansion against Sonny’s men.

## What Happens in “Keep Your Friends Close...” Mission?

The mission starts inside Tommy’s mansion. Sonny Forelli arrives with his gang, while Tommy realizes that his empire is under attack.

The first major objective is to protect the money stored inside the mansion. During the attack, several enemies enter the estate, and Tommy must fight through them while dealing with problems inside the building.

The mission then leads to a confrontation involving Lance Vance, who betrays Tommy. This betrayal changes the situation completely and makes the final battle much more difficult.

Eventually, Tommy faces Sonny in a final confrontation.

## How to Complete “Keep Your Friends Close...” in GTA Vice City?

Follow these steps to complete the final mission.

### 1. Prepare Your Weapons Before Starting

The final mission contains several intense firefights, so it is important to arrive properly equipped.

Useful weapons include:

- Assault rifle
- Shotgun
- Submachine gun
- Sniper rifle
- Body armor
- Plenty of ammunition

A strong automatic weapon is particularly useful because you will face multiple enemies inside the mansion.

If you have enough money, stock up on ammunition before entering the mission marker.

### 2. Defend the Vercetti Estate

Once the mission begins, enemies will attack the mansion.

Move through the estate carefully and eliminate enemies as they appear. Avoid standing in open areas for too long because several gang members can attack from different directions.

Use walls, doorways, and furniture as cover whenever possible.

Your mansion provides plenty of space to move around, so do not rush directly toward groups of enemies.

### 3. Deal With the Enemies Inside the Mansion

The attack eventually moves deeper into Tommy's estate.

Keep checking different rooms and hallways for enemies. Automatic weapons can make these encounters easier because you may need to eliminate several targets quickly.

If your health or armor gets low, take a moment to reposition rather than charging forward.

The goal is to survive the assault while keeping control of the mansion.

### 4. Watch Out for Lance Vance

One of the biggest surprises in the final mission is Lance Vance’s betrayal.

Lance turns against Tommy and becomes one of the major targets of the mission.

After the confrontation develops, chase Lance through the mansion and eliminate him.

Use an automatic weapon or shotgun at close range, but avoid wasting ammunition by firing continuously when he is moving away from you.

### 5. Chase Lance to the Rooftop

The confrontation with Lance eventually leads Tommy toward the upper areas of the mansion.

Follow him and continue attacking whenever you have a clear shot.

The rooftop area gives Lance room to move, so keep your weapon ready and avoid standing still.

Once Lance is defeated, the final confrontation with Sonny Forelli begins.

### 6. Defeat Sonny Forelli

Sonny is the final major enemy of the story.

After dealing with Lance, return your attention to Sonny and his men. Keep moving while firing at the enemies surrounding him.

Try to eliminate Sonny's nearby guards first if they are preventing you from getting a clear shot.

Once Sonny is defeated, the main conflict surrounding Tommy’s rise to power comes to an end.

## Tips to Complete the Final Mission Easily

The final mission can become much easier with the right preparation. Use these tips before starting:

### Stock Up on Armor

Body armor can make a major difference during the mansion shootout. Get full armor before beginning the mission.

### Carry Plenty of Ammunition

You will face numerous enemies, so running out of bullets can leave you vulnerable. Purchase ammunition for your strongest weapons beforehand.

### Use Cover

Do not remain in the open while enemies are shooting at you. Use the mansion's walls and doorways to reduce the amount of damage you take.

### Keep Moving

Standing still makes Tommy an easy target. Move between cover points while attacking enemies.

### Use Powerful Weapons

Automatic weapons are useful against groups of enemies. A shotgun can also be effective when enemies get close.

### Don't Rush the Final Fight

The mission contains several enemy waves. Take your time and eliminate opponents systematically instead of running into large groups.

## Is “Keep Your Friends Close...” the Final Mission?

Yes. “Keep Your Friends Close...” is the final main story mission in GTA Vice City.

It concludes Tommy Vercetti's main storyline and resolves his conflict with Sonny Forelli. The mission also reveals Lance Vance's betrayal and provides the final major confrontation at the Vercetti Estate.

After completing it, you can continue exploring Vice City and working toward additional completion goals, including remaining asset-related activities and collectibles.

## What Happens After Completing the Mission?

After Tommy defeats Sonny, the story reaches its conclusion. Tommy has successfully defended his empire and established himself as one of the most powerful figures in Vice City.

The ending also reinforces the importance of Tommy's relationship with Ken Rosenberg and his growing independence from the Forelli family.

Although the main storyline is finished, the game world remains open. You can continue playing, complete unfinished side activities, collect hidden packages, perform vehicle missions, and work toward 100% completion.

## Final Thoughts

“Keep Your Friends Close...” is one of the most memorable missions in GTA Vice City. The final battle combines betrayal, gunfights, and a dramatic confrontation between Tommy Vercetti and Sonny Forelli.

To complete it successfully, prepare your weapons and armor before entering the mission, use cover during the mansion attack, eliminate Lance when the betrayal occurs, and stay focused during the final fight with Sonny.

Once Sonny is defeated, Tommy's rise to power in Vice City reaches its dramatic conclusion.
