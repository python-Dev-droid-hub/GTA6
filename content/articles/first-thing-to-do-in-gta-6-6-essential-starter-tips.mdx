The single best first thing to do in [GTA 6](/) is to establish a daily workout routine at Jason’s safehouse in Key Lento, stash your loose cash under the floorboards, and complete your neighbor Shirley’s two opening errands before rushing the main story.

Unlike previous entries in the franchise, Grand Theft Auto VI introduces survival-style stat decay, strict police identification systems, and scarce early-game funds across the state of Leonida. Jumping straight into chaotic shootouts in Vice City will leave you broke and easily overwhelmed by revamped law enforcement AI. Following a smart opening routine sets up Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos with permanent combat buffs, reliable transportation, and clean Criminal Profiles from hour one.

## Your First 2 Hours in Leonida: Priority Checklist

When you take control of Jason in the Leonida Keys, follow these six steps in order to build momentum without wasting early resources:

### 1. Build a Daily Workout and Health Routine

Complete immediately at Jason's safehouse before free-roaming.

Grand Theft Auto VI tracks three core attributes: Health (pink heart), Stamina (blue lightning), and Focus (purple eye). Check Jason's kitchen cupboard for protein bars to temporarily boost health and reduce workout cooldowns. Run, bike, or kayak around Key Lento to raise Stamina—police taser hits immediately drain a base stamina bar, making early cardio essential for surviving chases. Meanwhile, low-intensity activities like fishing or shooting pool permanently increase your Focus bar, allowing you to slow down time longer during gunfights.

### 2. Stash Your Cash Under the Loose Floorboards

Critical protection against early-game arrests and deaths.

Until you unlock bank accounts later in the campaign, getting busted by Leonida police or wasted in a firefight strips away all the cash on your person along with any unowned weapons. Before leaving Key Lento, interact with the loose floorboards in Jason's bedroom to hide your savings. Carry only enough pocket change for immediate supplies like ammo or snacks.

### 3. Complete Neighbor Shirley’s Two Opening Errands

Unlocks a faster early-game vehicle.

As you step outside Jason's home during your first hour, your eccentric neighbor Shirley will call out asking you to pick up rum from the local liquor store. Finish both of her short favors right away. Doing so grants you free access to her sporty personal car, which handles significantly better than Jason's starter pickup truck—just drive carefully, as abandoning or totaling it prevents it from respawning.

### 4. Buy a Suppressor at the Key Lento Ammu-Nation

Available right after the introductory tutorial.

Ammo is expensive in the opening hours, and loud gunfights draw aggressive police responses. Head to the local Ammu-Nation in Key Lento and purchase a suppressor for your sidearm. Silent takedowns let you clear small groups of enemies one by one while keeping your Criminal Profile clean—a hidden system that tracks whether you use unnecessary lethal force during jobs.

### 5. Clear the Leonida Keys Underwater Dives

Takes 30–40 minutes for $2,000–$3,000 in early cash.

Once the story progresses into mainland Vice City, driving back down to the Leonida Keys takes roughly 10 minutes each way. Knock out the regional side activities while you are already stationed there. After meeting Kenton and completing the introductory dive, spend half an hour exploring the marked underwater treasure sites around the Keys. Selling those salvaged goods gives you an immediate $2,000 to $3,000 bankroll for weapon attachments and vehicle upgrades.

### 6. Learn Your Personal Vehicle Trunk Mechanics

Prevents permanent weapon loss.

Borrowing directly from Red Dead Redemption 2, your personal vehicle acts as a mobile armory and loot stash. Always stow rifles, shotguns, and robbery loot in your trunk. Any long gun stored inside your personal car becomes permanently bound to your inventory, meaning you can summon it anywhere via Joey without worrying about losing gear if you drop it during a chaotic escape.

## Core GTA 6 Starter Mechanics at a Glance

Mastering three new simulation layers early will save you hours of trial and error as the map opens up:

| Wanted & ID System | Police identify Jason and Lucia by face, outfit, vehicle license plate, and whether they are traveling together. | Wear a hat and bandana during robberies, keep a spare outfit in your trunk, and swap vehicles out of sight to drop Wanted levels fast. |

## Common Early-Game Mistakes to Avoid

Avoid hoarding unbanked cash early, as arrests wipe everything on your person—always use Jason’s floorboards. Never reuse recognized getaway cars or leave them parked in sight during robberies. Finally, clear the Leonida Keys underwater dives before advancing the main story to avoid a tedious ten-minute return drive later.

### Grinding for Millions in the Prologue

Early story events wipe or lock away portions of your initial cash flow. Invest your early earnings into permanent utility—like weapon attachments, suppressors, and safehouse garages once you leave Key Lento—rather than hoarding unbanked bills.

### Driving the Same Getaway Car Twice

Because law enforcement logs vehicle descriptions, driving a hot car back into a commercial district will trigger immediate recognition. Always park getaway vehicles out of sight behind buildings during stick-ups.

### Ignoring Ultimate Edition Unlocks

If you purchased the Ultimate Edition, claim your complimentary vehicles (such as the 1967 Vapid Dominator Buggy and Dinka Enduro dirt bike) and Watson Bay garage perks early so you have reliable transport across every terrain type in Leonida.

## Final Thoughts

Mastering your opening hours in Leonida sets the tone for your entire Grand Theft Auto VI journey. By prioritizing Jason’s daily workouts, hiding cash under the floorboards, finishing Shirley’s errands, and clearing the Leonida Keys underwater dives early, you build permanent stat advantages and steady funds without risking arrests. Follow these essential starter steps, stay smart around law enforcement, and enjoy taking over Vice City the right way.
