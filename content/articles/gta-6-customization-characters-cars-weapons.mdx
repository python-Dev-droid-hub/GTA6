[GTA 6](/) customization expands significantly beyond Grand Theft Auto V, allowing players to modify protagonists Jason and Lucia’s hairstyles, facial hair, makeup, nails, tattoos, clothing, and muscularity/weight via gyms and food. Beyond aesthetics, changing into full outfits stored in vehicle trunks actively clears police heat descriptions. Vehicle customization features six distinct categories—including full interior upholstery, exhaust acoustics, tunable suspension, and pre-made Pro Builds—while weapons support functional attachments like optical sights that enable first-person aiming.

Rockstar Games has transformed player personalization in Leonida from simple cosmetic swaps into systems tied directly to gameplay, immersion, and survival. While players step into the shoes of two fixed protagonists—Jason and Lucia—rather than creating a custom avatar from scratch, the depth of control over how they look, train, dress, and drive is the most comprehensive in the franchise's history.

## Character Customization: Jason and Lucia

Following the GTA V template, you cannot alter Jason or Lucia’s facial bone structure or skin tone, as both are authored narrative leads. However, Rockstar gives players extensive control over their day-to-day appearance:

### Two-Tier Hair System

Grooming is split between your home base and commercial shops. At your safehouse, grooming is strictly reductive—you can trim hair shorter or shave it off completely. To get a brand-new cut or styled look, you must visit barbers and hair salons across Leonida, each carrying exclusive signature styles.

### Grooming & Body Art

Players can customize Jason's facial hair, switch up Lucia’s makeup and nail styles, and apply over 50+ tattoos at parlors.

### Gyms, Muscle, and Weight

Returning from San Andreas, physique management plays a major role. Jason and Lucia can work out at gyms—even training together—to progress through distinct levels of muscularity and strength. Eating food also affects body weight while acting as a temporary stat buff.

## Why Outfits Matter for Escaping the Police?

In GTA 6, clothing is a tactical tool. Under the overhauled law enforcement system, police build an active description of suspects based on physical appearance, clothing, vehicle, and whether Jason and Lucia are traveling together.

- Putting on a hat, sunglasses, or getting a quick haircut will not fool pursuing officers if you are wearing the same clothes.
- Changing into a completely new outfit breaks the police description and clears active heat.
- Players can store spare outfits directly inside the trunk of their personal vehicles, allowing quick mid-escape wardrobe swaps without needing to reach a safehouse or clothing store.

## Vehicle Customization: Interiors, Acoustics, and Pro Builds

Car culture in Vice City and greater Leonida takes center stage with a two-tier garage system: basic spray shops for quick color changes, and dedicated mod shops for comprehensive mechanical and visual overhauls. Before modifying a stolen street car, players must use the in-game Waink app to check its security system and pay to register it if it carries a standard tracker.

Mod shops organize upgrades across six main categories:

### Interior Customization

For the first time in the mainline series, players can overhaul vehicle cabins. Options include swapping seats, steering wheels, dashboard trim (Stock, Classic, Black, Vintage), stitching colors, and multi-zone upholstery materials (leather, cloth, or hybrid layouts).

### Performance & Tuning

Engine swaps, transmissions, boost systems, and induction kits (stock, supercharger, or turbo—each producing distinct audio profiles) directly impact five vehicle stats: Speed, Acceleration, Asphalt Handling, Off-Road Handling, and Strength. Raising suspension clearance and adding off-road tires measurably improves dirt-road grip.

### Bodywork & Functional Parts

Customize hoods, hood pins, splitters, bumpers, seven grades of window tint, headlights (Warm Halogen, White/Yellow LEDs), and exhausts that alter engine note. Certain parts add utility, such as functional tow hitches on trucks like Jason’s Vapid Ganado.

### Cosmetics & Dual Liveries

Alongside paint finishes and license plates, vehicles support Curated Liveries (pre-designed wraps) and Customizable Liveries where players adjust stripe layouts and individual color zones.

### Wheels

Modify rim styles, wheel diameters, and tire profiles.

### Pro Builds

If you want a cohesive look without picking individual parts, mod shops offer pre-packaged Pro Builds (such as Retro Build – Standard or Elite) that bundle matching exterior paint, interior trim, and engine tuning at a set price.

## Weapon & Loadout Customization

Gunplay customization blends visual flair with mechanical utility. Beyond cosmetic finishes and exclusive weapon patterns, weapon attachments directly change how firearms handle in combat:

### Optical Sights

Equipping a scope or reflex sight unlocks the ability to click the right stick and aim down sights (ADS) in first-person perspective—a feature unavailable on standard iron sights.

### Trunk Arsenal Storage

Because players can no longer carry dozens of heavy weapons invisibly on their person, personal vehicle trunks double as mobile armories where you store and swap your customized loadouts alongside spare clothes.

## Final Thoughts

GTA 6 customization elevates personalization from simple cosmetics into meaningful survival and performance mechanics. Whether you are training Jason and Lucia at Leonida gyms, storing heat-clearing outfits in your trunk, or fine-tuning cabin upholstery and suspension dynamics, every choice shapes how you interact with Vice City. By blending deep visual expression with tactical gameplay utility, Rockstar Games delivers the most immersive and reactive sandbox customization in franchise history.
