When Rockstar Games revealed the official footage for Grand Theft Auto VI, gamers around the world caught their first real glimpse of the series' next-generation setting: the State of Leonida.

Moving far beyond the localized boundaries of a single metropolitan area, GTA 6 introduces an entire virtual U.S. state inspired by modern-day Florida. Centered around the neon-drenched metropolis of Vice City, Leonida promises to deliver Rockstar’s largest and most detail-packed sandbox environment to date.

Below is an in-depth breakdown of everything we know about the GTA 6 map, including scale estimates, key regions, and breakthrough gameplay features.

## How Big Is the GTA 6 Map?

The GTA 6 map is set to redefine open-world scale, covering the expansive fictional State of Leonida. Industry previews and community mapping projects estimate that the world is roughly twice the size of GTA 5 and nearly three times larger than Red Dead Redemption 2. Beyond raw geographical acreage, Rockstar has packed this massive landscape with unprecedented interior access, intricate environmental details, and deeply reactive populations.

### Double the Size of Los Santos

When compared directly to GTA 5, the sheer scale of the State of Leonida is astounding. The new map effectively doubles the playable landmass of Los Santos and Blaine County combined. Players will experience a far wider variety of distinct biomes, moving seamlessly from urban concrete jungles to expansive rural territories and vast coastal waters.

### A Massive Urban Footprint

Vice City itself has grown into a towering powerhouse. The central urban core is reported to be nearly double the size of Los Santos on its own. Packed with bustling high-rises, vibrant beach strips, and dense commercial districts, the city offers an unparalleled level of architectural depth and accessible indoor locations for players to explore.

### Vast Horizon

Beyond the central city limits lies a vast, untamed horizon full of geographical diversity. Leonida stretches seamlessly into swampy bayous, secluded island chains connected by long overseas highways, and dense northern state parks. This immense geographical variety ensures that long-distance travel, high-speed chases, and off-road exploration feel more open and rewarding than ever before.

### Vice City (The Urban Core)

Vice City stands as the glowing heart of Leonida, blending high-stakes luxury with a relentless criminal underworld. Drawing heavy inspiration from modern Miami, this sprawling metropolis is defined by towering skyscrapers, sun-soaked coastal boulevards, and vibrant culturally rich neighborhoods. Every district feels alive with dense pedestrian traffic, reactive emergency services, and seamlessly enterable storefronts, delivering an unparalleled urban sandbox that serves as the main focal point of the story.

### Ocean Beach & Washington Beach

Ocean Beach and Washington Beach capture the iconic coastal glamour of Vice City. Lined with classic Art Deco architecture, pastel-colored boutique hotels, and wide sandy shores, these bustling beachfront strips are packed with sunbathers, luxury sports cars, and street performers. At night, the entire coastline transforms into a stunning, neon-lit playground for tourists and local nightlife enthusiasts alike.

### Downtown Vice City

Downtown Vice City serves as the state’s financial and corporate epicentre. Dominated by glass skyscrapers like the Megamundo Tower, this high-density urban district houses corporate headquarters, luxury penthouse suites, and sprawling commercial plazas. High-speed highway flyovers and tight urban alleys create a fast-paced environment perfect for intense police pursuits, high-stakes robberies, and risky getaway escapes.

### Nightlife & Clubs

Vice City truly comes alive after dark through its expansive nightlife ecosystem. Legendary venues such as the Jack of Hearts nightclub feature fully rendered indoor spaces packed with dynamic lighting, deep bass, and complex crowd behaviors. From high-end VIP lounges to underground dives, these venues offer players immersive social hubs, story encounters, and lucrative illegal opportunities after hours.

## The Leonida Keys (The Southern Islands)

Based on the real-world Florida Keys, this tropical archipelago stretches south of the mainland, connected by long overseas highway spans:

- Ideal for deep-sea boating, jet-skiing, and coastal exploration.
- Home to isolated coastal compounds and maritime trade routes.

## Grassrivers (The Swamplands)

A modern take on the Florida Everglades, Grassrivers brings an untamed wilderness biome to the center of the map:

- Navigable via high-speed airboats through dense mangrove channels.
- Teeming with dangerous local wildlife, including alligators and snakes.
- Features off-grid settlements and secluded illegal operations hidden in the marshes.

## Port Gellhorn, Ambrosia, and the North

Moving away from the coastal glitz, the northern and western reaches of Leonida show the industrial and rural side of the state:

### Port Gellhorn

Port Gellhorn serves as the gritty, blue-collar industrial hub of Leonida. Far removed from the neon glow of Vice City, this working-class town features sprawling shipping yards, manufacturing facilities, and local strip malls. It offers a rugged environment filled with dirt bike tracks, industrial hideouts, and tight backstreets, giving players a distinct change of pace and tactical landscape for high-speed getaways.

### Ambrosia

Ambrosia represents the rustic agricultural and manufacturing heartland of the State of Leonida. This sprawling rural zone features vast farmland plots, quiet worker townships, and small-town commerce. Surrounded by expansive open roads and industrial processing plants, Ambrosia provides a stark contrast to the urban sprawl, creating ideal terrain for rural highway chases, smuggling runs, and off-grid exploration.

### Mount Kalaga National Park

Nestled along the northern border, Mount Kalaga National Park is a rugged wilderness sanctuary offering breathtaking natural scenery. Featuring dense evergreen forests, steep mountain trails, and winding river systems, it provides endless opportunities for hunting, fishing, and off-road driving. This remote highland region adds incredible verticality and untamed danger to the overall map design of Leonida.

## Next-Generation Map Features

Rockstar has introduced several structural advancements to elevate how players interact with the map:

### Highly Accessible Interiors

Unlike older titles where most buildings served as static backdrops, GTA 6 introduces seamless entry into a far higher percentage of structures. Players can walk directly into gas stations, diners, pawn shops, and residential complexes without loading screens.

### Reactive NPC Populations & Weather

The world reacts dynamically to time and weather. Beaches empty out during sudden rainstorms, highway traffic builds up during peak commute times, and local fauna responds realistically to human activity.

### Advanced Navigation

The in-game radar and pause map utilize high-resolution satellite topography. This design helps players easily spot back alleys, waterways, shortcuts, and off-road escape routes during police pursuits.

## Final Thoughts

The State of Leonida is set to revolutionize open-world gaming by combining unprecedented map scale with unparalleled environmental detail. From the neon-drenched streets of Vice City to the rugged wilderness of Mount Kalaga, GTA 6 offers a dynamic, living ecosystem packed with dense interiors and reactive wildlife. Whether you are executing high-stakes robberies or exploring vast swamplands, Leonida promises an endless, immersive playground that will define gaming for years to come.
