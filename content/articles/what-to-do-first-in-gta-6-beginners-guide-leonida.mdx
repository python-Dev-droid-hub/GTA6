The best thing to do first in [GTA 6](/) is follow the opening story until you can leave Jason’s house and explore freely. Then get familiar with the house, stash any spare cash, and take a short drive around the Leonida Keys. Once you know your way back, try a local side activity or return to the main story to open up more things to do.

## Finish the Opening Missions Before You Explore

The prologue gives you a guided introduction before free roam begins. Follow the story markers and use the early missions to get comfortable with driving, moving on foot, and reading the HUD. Your first useful landmark is Jason’s house. Once you can leave on your own, you have a base for your first trip around the Keys.

If you’re eager to head to Vice City, start with one local detour and then pick up the next story mission. Some activities, including treasure dives, open up through story progress. A short trip around the Keys gives you room to experiment while keeping those early unlocks within reach.

## Get to Know Jason’s House and Protect Your Cash

Before your first drive, check the loose floorboards in Jason’s bedroom. You can stash cash there before the Buck Me mission unlocks a bank account. Money you carry can be lost if Jason dies or gets arrested, so leave behind what you’re saving for a later purchase.

Then try the workout equipment on the porch or shoot a few hoops nearby. These are quick ways to see how ordinary activities affect character stats. Weight training builds strength, and shooting hoops can improve Focus. Try one while you’re at the house, then head out.

## Take a Short Drive Around the Leonida Keys

Use Jason’s truck for a simple first route. Pick a nearby shop or waterfront stop, drive there, and find your way back to the house. That short trip gives you a chance to learn the controls and recognize the roads you’ll use again during the opening chapter.

For your next stop, look for a darker blue building on the world map. VGC’s launch guide reports that this shading indicates a building has an accessible interior. As you get closer, the minimap can help you locate an entrance. If a shop is closed, check the time on your in-game phone and try again during the day.

## Try a Side Activity and Earn Early Money

Help Shirley if she calls out near Jason’s house. Her first errand gives you a destination close to home and a chance to get familiar with the neighborhood. It’s a good first detour because you can pick it up while you’re already exploring the area.

Try treasure diving once the story unlocks it. After the early boat outing with Kenton, look for additional dive sites. You can recover valuables while exploring underwater, making treasure dives a practical way to earn money early in GTA 6. If the activity hasn’t appeared yet, continue with the opening missions.

## Explore Leonida One Region at a Time

Once you’re ready to venture farther, choose a destination based on what you want to see. Ocean Beach gives you a recognizable introduction to Vice City. Grassrivers offers wetlands, and Mount Kalaga National Park gives you a reason to head inland. Use these as destinations for later outings as you progress through the story.

| Destination | What to Do There |
| --- | --- |
| Leonida Keys | Learn the roads around Jason’s house, try a local errand, and return for treasure dives once they unlock. |
| Ocean Beach | Park near the beachfront and explore the Art Deco strip on foot. |
| Grassrivers | Plan a separate outing to explore Leonida’s wetlands. |
| Mount Kalaga National Park | Take an inland trip to see a different side of the state. |

## Conclusion

Start GTA 6 by finishing the opening missions, learning your way around Jason’s house, and taking a short drive through the Leonida Keys. Stash spare cash, help Shirley when her errand appears, and try treasure diving once it unlocks. Then choose one new destination and give yourself time to explore.
