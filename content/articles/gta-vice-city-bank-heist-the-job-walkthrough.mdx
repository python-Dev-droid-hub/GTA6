To pull off the [GTA Vice City](/) bank heist (officially titled "The Job"), you must purchase the Malibu Club for $120,000, complete three setup missions to recruit your crew, and infiltrate El Banco Corrupto Grande in Little Havana. Once inside, secure the bank manager to crack the vault, fight off an eight-man VCPD SWAT ambush, and drive your surviving crew to the nearby Little Haiti Pay 'n' Spray before returning to Cam’s safe house.

Inspired heavily by Michael Mann’s 1995 crime thriller Heat, the GTA Vice City bank heist is the crowning achievement of Tommy Vercetti’s criminal empire. Unlike modern Grand Theft Auto titles that offer dynamic heist planning boards, Vice City executes its robbery through a linear, high-stakes narrative arc that tests your preparation, crowd control, and getaway driving.

## Mission Overview & Quick Facts

## Phase 1: Assembling the Heist Crew (Setup Missions)

Before you can step foot inside El Banco Corrupto Grande, you must buy the Malibu Club in Vice Point and complete three prerequisite setup missions:

### No Escape? (Recruiting the Safecracker)

Infiltrate the Washington Beach police station, steal the cell keycard, and break Cam Jones out of jail. Cam is the only safecracker in Vice City capable of opening the bank’s vault.

### The Shootist (Recruiting the Gunman)

Meet Phil Cassidy at the Downtown Ammu-Nation firing range and beat his high score of 60 points to prove your marksmanship and earn his loyalty.

### The Driver (Recruiting the Wheelman)

Challenge Hilary King to an illegal street race across Vice Point while evading a 2-star wanted level. Winning convinces Hilary to serve as your getaway driver.

## Phase 2: Pre-Heist Preparation Checklist

In both the original 2002 release and GTA: The Trilogy – The Definitive Edition, "The Job" lacks mid-mission checkpoints. Failing outside the bank forces you to replay the entire drive and cutscene sequence. Prepare with these loadouts before walking into the Malibu Club marker:

### Recommended Weapons

Equip an M4 or Kruger assault rifle for precision headshots against SWAT officers, and bring a Minigun or Rocket Launcher (unlocked via Hidden Packages) to instantly neutralize SWAT Enforcer vans during the street escape.

### Max Health & Armor

Complete Level 10 of the Pizza Boy side mission for 150 Max Health and Level 12 of Vigilante for 150 Max Armor.

### Drive Carefully to the Bank

You must drive the crew to Little Havana in a standard Taxi. Do not wreck the vehicle on the way—Hilary drives this exact Taxi around the block and uses it for your extraction.

## Phase 3: Step-by-Step Bank Heist Walkthrough

Storm El Banco Corrupto Grande, eliminate security guards, and escort the branch manager to unlock the vault. Survive the VCPD SWAT ambush, hijack your getaway taxi, and hit Pay 'n' Spray.

### Infiltrate the Lobby and Reach the Vault

Pull up across the street from El Banco Corrupto Grande and step into the pink marker next to the entrance. Tommy, Phil, and Cam will change into green jumpsuits and hockey masks before storming the lobby.

Leave Phil downstairs to watch the hostages and follow Cam up the stairs. Eliminate the Patrol Invest Group (PIG) security guards stationed in the upper offices and take the elevator down to the vault floor. Clear the hallway guard and step into the marker by the vault door.

### Retrieve the Bank Manager

Cam will realize the vault requires a override code from the branch manager. Take the elevator back up to the office level.

**Pro Tip:** Before entering the manager's office at the end of the hall, duck into the security room on your left to grab a Body Armor pickup.

Eliminate the guard inside the manager's office, grab the Health pickup behind his desk if needed, and escort the manager back down the elevator to Cam.

### Survive the VCPD SWAT Ambush

While Cam forces the manager to open the vault, head back to the upper lobby balcony to check on Phil. A guard triggers the silent alarm, and VCPD SWAT teams breach the building.

- Take cover near the top of the staircase rather than standing in the open lobby.
- When SWAT officers rappel down through the ceiling smoke grenades, pick them off with your M4 before they hit the ground.
- Clear all eight SWAT officers—including the straggler who occasionally lingers on the upper mezzanine—before regrouping with Phil and Cam.

### Execute the Getaway

Step outside the front doors to trigger a brief cutscene where Hilary arrives in the Taxi—only to be gunned down immediately by waiting SWAT units.

**Critical AI Pathing Tip:** Walk down the front steps instead of jumping over the concrete railing. Jumping confuses Phil and Cam's pathfinding AI, causing them to get stuck outside while under heavy fire.

Clear the SWAT officers flanking the Taxi, wait for Phil to enter the vehicle (Cam's survival is optional after the vault opens, though keeping him alive cuts your payout from $50,000 to $30,000), and speed north toward Little Haiti.

### Lose the 4-Star Wanted Level

Turn left onto the main avenue and drive straight into the Little Haiti Pay 'n' Spray, located roughly two blocks north of the bank. Hug the wide sidewalks to dodge police spike strips and SWAT roadblocks. Once your 4-star wanted level clears, drive south to Cam’s Can Openers in Little Havana to complete the heist.

## Final Thoughts

Pulling off the GTA Vice City bank heist rewards you with $50,000, the iconic Bank Job outfit, and $10,000 in daily Malibu Club revenue. With smart preparation, heavy firepower, and a clean Pay 'n' Spray getaway, you will conquer El Banco Corrupto Grande and cement Tommy Vercetti’s criminal empire.
