Driving across the state of Leonida requires far more precision than simply holding down the gas pedal. [Grand Theft Auto VI](/) blends heavy vehicle inertia with grounded traction physics, punishing reckless speed and rewarding clean throttle control. Whether you are weaving through dense Vice City traffic or outrunning a six-star wanted level on wet coastal highways, mastering your car's weight is essential.

Before hitting top speeds, fine-tuning your settings and learning core cornering techniques will keep your vehicle intact and off the guardrails.

## Optimize Your Driving Settings First

Default controller settings in GTA 6 can make high-speed steering feel twitchy and braking overly abrupt. Adjusting a few key options immediately improves vehicle control:

### Adjust Left Stick Deadzone

The default deadzone can cause cars to veer sharply with tiny thumb movements. Lowering the deadzone slider to 2 or 3 gives you smoother micro-adjustments on straightaways and sweeping bends.

### Turn Off Adaptive Triggers Resistance (PS5)

Default trigger resistance creates a stiff midpoint wall on R2 and L2, making acceleration and braking feel like an on-off switch. Disabling this setting lets you feather the throttle and apply progressive brake pressure.

### Switch Nitrous to Press Mode

Setting nitrous to a single press instead of holding allows you to trigger boosts cleanly on straight exits without compromising your grip mid-corner.

### Disable Cinematic Crash Cameras

Setting crash camera frequency to zero prevents slow-motion replays from breaking your line of sight during intense chases.

## Mastering High-Speed Turns

Unlike the arcade-style grip of older titles, vehicles in GTA 6 carry real momentum. Entering a 90-degree Vice City intersection at full throttle will send you straight into oncoming traffic.

### Brake Before the Apex

Slow down in a straight line before turning the wheel. Hard braking while steering locks your tires and triggers severe understeer, pushing the nose of your car wide. Apply smooth brake pressure early, coast through the corner's apex, and accelerate once your front tires point toward the exit.

### Use Controlled Handbrake Drifts

For hairpin turns or sudden U-turns during police pursuits, tap the handbrake while initiating your turn. Avoid holding the handbrake down for too long, which kills momentum and causes spin-outs. A quick tap breaks rear traction just enough to swing the tail around, allowing you to power out of the slide with counter-steering.

### Respect Drivetrain Differences

Every vehicle class behaves differently on the road:

**Rear-Wheel Drive (RWD)**  
Muscle cars and classic cruisers kick their rear ends out easily under heavy throttle. Feather the gas when exiting corners.

**All-Wheel Drive (AWD)**  
Sports sedans and modern supercars offer superior traction and recover faster from slides, making them ideal for tight city grids.

## Surviving Dangerous Roads and Heavy Traffic

Leonida's road network shifts rapidly from congested downtown avenues to narrow, uneven backroads. Use these habits to survive unpredictable hazards:

### Look Ahead, Not at the Minimap

Staring at the GPS route causes late reactions. Scan traffic several cars ahead to spot lane mergers and sudden stops early.

### Manage Bumps and Elevation Changes

Hitting crests or uneven pavement at top speed unloads your suspension and strips away steering grip. Lift off the accelerator slightly before driving over steep hills or railroad tracks to keep all four tires planted.

### Use Turn Signals in Dense Traffic

Tapping left or right on the D-pad activates your blinkers. Surprisingly, nearby NPC drivers often recognize the signal and yield space when you need to merge cleanly through gridlock. ### Duck During Ambush Drives

When taking heavy gunfire on open stretches, hold the duck button to lower your character behind the dashboard, reducing headshot risks while maintaining lane control.

## Quick Reference: Cornering and Road Recovery

| Driving Situation | Recommended Technique | Common Mistake to Avoid |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sharp City Corner | Brake early in a straight line, then steer smoothly | Braking hard while turning the wheel |
| Tight Hairpin / U-Turn | Tap handbrake briefly and counter-steer out | Holding the handbrake and stalling momentum |
| Wet or Uneven Backroads | Feather throttle and avoid sudden steering jerks | Keeping full throttle over road crests |
| Flipped or Stuck Vehicle | Exit and push from the side to flip it upright | Wasting time trying to rock the car with steering |

Mastering high-speed driving in GTA 6 comes down to patience, smooth inputs, and respecting your vehicle's weight.
