Grand Theft Auto 6 drops players into an ultra-realistic, modernized iteration of Leonida (Vice City and its surrounding wetlands, keys, and highways), and the sheer scale of the map can cause instant paralysis of choice. When the prologue wraps up and the open world finally breathes life around you, heading straight for a main story mission is a rookie trap. Rockstar has engineered a playground with unprecedented systemic depth, meaning your absolute first hour should be spent setting up your criminal empire's foundation and learning how the new mechanics operate.

> **Focus:** **what to do first in GTA 6** — vehicles, safehouses, social media, early robberies, and priority upgrades.

## Secure a Reliable Vehicle and Stash Location

The physics engine in GTA 6 completely overhauls vehicle handling, vehicle wear, and pedestrian AI, making driving a high-stakes affair. Police response times are sharper, and witnesses will actively record your crimes on their phones and upload them to the in-game social media feed before calling authorities.

### Avoid High-End Supercars Initially

Stealing a flashy hypercar right out of the gate draws immediate, aggressive police heat. Leonida’s updated pedestrian AI and hyper-realistic witness mechanics mean bystanders will film your crimes instantly, uploading them to the social media feed before cops even arrive. Keep a low profile with standard traffic to survive the opening hours.

### Prioritize Utility or Off-Road Vehicles

Vice City features unpredictable coastal weather, muddy swamps, and rugged backroads. Swapping sports cars for an SUV, pickup, or airboat ensures you never get stranded during a fast escape. These durable rides handle rough terrain effortlessly, keeping you out of reach from pursuing local authorities and rival crews.

### Locate a Safehouse Garage

As soon as early map exploration unlocks, secure a discreet motel garage or hideout. Storing a customized ride prevents you from permanently losing rare vehicles when you die or get busted. Having a dedicated stash point also shields your hard-earned cash and early gear from high-stakes confiscation during early police encounters.

## Explore the Leonida Social Media Feed

Unlike previous titles where you discovered side content entirely through map icons or random encounters, GTA 6 ties a massive portion of its emergent gameplay, side hustles, and underground contacts to the in-game social media app on your character's smartphone.

### Scroll the Feeds

Spend ten to fifteen minutes reading local posts, viral videos, and regional rumors. Citizens post about illegal street races, unique vehicle spawns, and unprotected local businesses.

### Bookmark Points of Interest

Pinning these social media locations directly to your GPS creates a customized, organic route for early cash-grabs and weapon upgrades before the main campaign forces your hand.

## Invest in Stealth and Store-Robbery Mechanics

Early-game cash flow dictates how quickly you can buy ammunition, body armor, and property. While major heists are saved for later acts, minor criminal enterprises are available from minute one.

### Test the New Convenience Store Robberies

Cashiers now exhibit dynamic fear metrics, and you can hold them at gunpoint while bagging cash yourself, or talk your way out of tight spots if police sirens start wailing nearby.

### Master the Surrender and Escape System

Learn how to ditch your outer layers of clothing or swap vehicles quickly to drop your wanted level. Getting caught early drains critical starting cash on hospital and bail fees.

## Unlocks and Upgrades to Prioritize

Your character starts with bare-bones inventory limits and primitive weapons. Before touching the second main story mission, take time to visit local merchants and gun shops to get your bearings.

| Upgrade Category | What to Focus On? | Why It Matters Early? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Weapons & Gear | Suppressed sidearm & heavy body armor | Minimizes noise during early skirmishes and prevents instant death from reactive police squads. |
| Stamina & Fitness | Gyms and endurance activities | Running out of breath during foot pursuits in Leonida's heat will cost you your freedom. |
| Map Intelligence | Convenience stores, gas stations, and gun shops | Unlocks fast-travel routes, quick supply restocks, and local safe zones. |

## Final Thoughts

Jumping straight into the main story of Grand Theft Auto 6 is a recipe for early frustration. By taking the time to secure durable utility vehicles, bypass flashy supercars, scope out local social media trends, and lock down a secure safehouse, you build a bulletproof foundation for your criminal enterprise. Mastering these early mechanics ensures you navigate the dangers of Leonida with maximum profit and minimal police interference.
