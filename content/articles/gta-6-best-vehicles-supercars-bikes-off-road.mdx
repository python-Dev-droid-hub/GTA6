With the return to Vice City and the state of Leonida, vehicle customization, handling physics, and garage selection are set for a major leap forward. From high-octane highway racing along Ocean Drive to tearing through mud pits in the Gator Keys, vehicle selection will dictate your gameplay experience.

Here is a breakdown of the top sports cars, supercars, bikes, and off-road vehicles confirmed and teased for Grand Theft Auto VI.

## High-Performance Supercars & Exotics

Vice City's neon-lit coastal strips demand top-tier speed and sharp handling. Supercars deliver peak acceleration for quick getaways across Leonida's highways.

### Grotti Furia

**Real-World Inspiration:** Ferrari SF90 Stradale

**Vehicle Class:** Supercar

**Best For:** High-speed highway getaways and sharp cornering.

**Why Watch It:** The mid-engine layout paired with active aerodynamics makes the Furia a top pick for tight urban racing and outrunning law enforcement.

### Pegassi Tempesta

**Real-World Inspiration:** Lamborghini Huracán

**Vehicle Class:** Supercar

**Best For:** All-wheel-drive traction on slick coastal pavement.

**Why Watch It:** Excellent launch control and launch stability ensure high control during aggressive street pursuits.

### Pegassi Zorrusso

**Real-World Inspiration:** Pagani Huayra / Design Concepts

**Vehicle Class:** Hypercar / Roadster

**Best For:** Open-top cruising and peak top speeds.

**Why Watch It:** Combines high-end cosmetic customizability with exceptional straight-line speed.

## Street Sports Cars & Classic Tuners

Sports cars offer the ideal balance between raw horsepower and everyday usability, making them prime candidates for deep performance upgrades.

### Bravado Banshee

A franchise staple inspired by the Dodge Viper. Expect aggressive rear-wheel-drive drift dynamics suited for city corners.

### Grotti Cheetah Classic

Channels vintage 1980s Miami vibes, perfect for players looking to match Vice City's classic aesthetic.

## Motorcycles & Street Bikes

Motorcycles offer high mobility through dense traffic and tight alleys, though they carry higher risk during combat.

### Principe Alvino V1

Inspired by modern Ducati superbikes like the Panigale V2, the Principe Alvino V1 delivers explosive acceleration and razor-sharp handling. Its lightweight frame and high-revving engine make it an absolute track monster on Vice City's open highways. Perfect for lane-splitting through dense traffic during high-speed police getaways, this sportbike balances extreme top speeds with precise cornering performance.

### Shitzu PCJ-600

A true franchise staple, the Shitzu PCJ-600 draws heavy inspiration from classic Japanese inline-four street bikes. It offers a well-rounded mix of rapid throttle response, mid-range speed, and nimble maneuvering across tight urban gridlock. Whether you are cutting through narrow back alleys or weaving between lanes on Vice City avenues, its reliable agility makes it a top getaway choice.

### Western Zombie Chopper

Modeled after traditional Harley-Davidson Softail customs, the Western Zombie Chopper embodies raw American muscle and long-distance cruising style. Featuring an extended front fork, deep-rumbling V-twin engine, and low-slung seating position, this motorcycle prioritizes straight-line stability over sharp turns. It remains the quintessential ride for cruising Leonida's coastal highways and repping outlaw biker culture in style.

### Dinka Enduro

Modeled after rugged dual-purpose dirt bikes like the Kawasaki KLR, the Dinka Enduro seamlessly bridges the gap between pavement and dirt. High ground clearance and long-travel suspension allow it to absorb harsh bumps effortlessly, making it the ultimate multi-terrain machine for transitioning from city streets to muddy Leonida backroads.

## Off-Road Vehicles & Trail Monsters

The wetlands, swamps, and dirt trails of Leonida require specialized suspension, high ground clearance, and all-wheel-drive systems.

### Nagasaki Outlaw (UTV)

Modeled after the high-performance Can-Am Maverick X3, the Nagasaki Outlaw is an agile, ultra-lightweight utility task vehicle. Its exposed roll-cage design and long-travel suspension absorb heavy landings effortlessly across rugged terrain. Perfect for tearing through sand dunes or navigating dense, muddy trails, this UTV ensures maximum control and continuous momentum where traditional street vehicles quickly stall out.

### Vapid Dominator Buggy

Fusing classic American muscle car power with heavy-duty off-road engineering, the Vapid Dominator Buggy features a lifted, reinforced chassis built for raw destruction. Powered by a high-torque V8 engine, it effortlessly plows through barricades and harsh environments alike. It provides unmatched durability, high ground clearance, and relentless speed when escaping law enforcement through Leonida's rough backwoods.

### Canis Kamacho / Caracara 4x4

Drawing design cues from rugged military trucks and heavy-duty Ford Raptors, these full-sized 4x4 pickups dominate challenging landscapes. Outfitted with aggressive all-terrain tires, robust four-wheel-drive systems, and heavy towing capability, they are built to ram through road blocks and haul gear. They serve as reliable, indestructible workhorses whether traversing deep mud pits or asphalt highways.

### Swamp Buggies & Airboats

Engineered specifically for the shallow, weed-choked waters of Leonida's Everglades-inspired wetlands, these specialized vehicles offer unmatched aquatic mobility. Featuring high-mounted fan propulsion and massive oversized tires, they glide effortlessly across water, mud, and marshland where conventional boats and off-road trucks instantly get bogged down.

## Key Takeaway for Garage Management

Selecting the right vehicle depends heavily on terrain. Keep a Supercar on standby in downtown Vice City garages for quick urban transit, store a Dual-Sport Bike for agile city-to-trail travel, and maintain a dedicated Off-Road Truck or Buggy at rural safehouses to handle the mud of outer Leonida.
