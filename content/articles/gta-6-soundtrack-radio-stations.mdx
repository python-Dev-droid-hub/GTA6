Few elements define the Grand Theft Auto franchise quite like its radio stations. Cruising down Ocean Drive with the windows down and neon lights reflecting off the hood requires the perfect soundtrack.

With Grand Theft Auto 6 bringing players back to a modern-day Vice City (and the broader state of Leonida), music expectations are at an all-time high.

From iconic 80s nostalgia to modern Miami trap, Latin dembow, and classic rock, here is a complete breakdown of what’s already confirmed for the GTA 6 soundtrack—and what players are begging Rockstar Games to include on the dial.

## What We Know So Far: Confirmed GTA 6 Songs & Leaked Stations

Rockstar Games gave fans their first taste of Vice City’s musical tone in the official trailers and gameplay previews. Highlights include:

### Tom Petty – "Love Is a Long Road"

Featured prominently in the official reveal trailer, Tom Petty’s classic track instantly established the atmospheric tone for GTA 6. Beyond its nostalgic Florida-rock roots, the song perfectly mirrors the high-stakes narrative and complex relationship dynamics between protagonists Jason and Lucia. It sets an authentic local backdrop while promising an epic, story-driven highway ride across the state of Leonida.

### Phil Collins – "Against All Odds (Take a Look at Me Now)"

Phil Collins holds a legendary legacy within the franchise, famously appearing in GTA: Vice City Stories. The inclusion of this emotional, powerful ballad signals a deep respect for series history. Delivering dramatic 80s flair, the track brings high-energy emotion and cinematic weight to late-night drives through Vice City’s glowing, neon-drenched streets during key storyline moments.

### Modern Bangers & Hip-Hop

Vice City's contemporary sound relies heavily on modern hip-hop, trapping, and high-energy club anthems. Featuring heavy hitters like Travis Scott, Sexyy Red, Kodak Black, and Metro Boomin, the radio dial captures the vibrant energy of today’s Florida music scene. These tracks supply an upbeat, modern backdrop for fast-paced street chases, heist planning, and cruising through bustling urban nightlife.

### Eclectic Hits

To keep the airwaves diverse and surprising, Rockstar incorporates a wide mix of genres. From synth-pop icons like Depeche Mode and Wang Chung to classic country vibes and Latin rhythms, these eclectic tracks ensure every neighborhood in Leonida feels uniquely alive, distinct, and culturally rich as you explore.

## 5 Things Players Want to Hear on the Vice City Airwaves

While the confirmed tracklist is off to a strong start, the community has high hopes for the full release. Here is what players are hoping to hear on the GTA 6 radio wheel:

### The Ultimate 1980s Nostalgia Channel

The original GTA: Vice City (2002) immortalized the 1980s synth-wave era. Even though GTA 6 takes place in present day, fans overwhelmingly want a dedicated throwback station—or a revived Flash FM—packed with Michael Jackson, Hall & Oates, Laura Branigan, and Duran Duran to relive that synth-pop magic.

### Authentic Miami Bass and Southern Rap

Vice City wouldn't be complete without paying homage to the Florida rap scene that shaped the region. Fans are looking for a deep dive into 90s Miami Bass—think 2 Live Crew and Poison Clan—alongside modern Florida heavyweights like Denzel Curry, Rick Ross, Kodak Black, and City Girls.

### Expanded Latin & Caribbean Stations

Miami and Southern Florida represent a vibrant fusion of Cuban, Haitian, Dominican, and Puerto Rican cultures. Beyond mainstream reggaeton, players want dedicated stations for Salsa, Bachata, Soca, and Dancehall, capturing the authentic sound of Vice City's neighborhoods.

### Hilarious Parody Talk Radio (VCPR & K-CHAT Returns)

Grand Theft Auto radio isn’t just about the music—it’s about the satirical commercials and eccentric talk show hosts. Fans are calling for the return of iconic talk shows like Vice City Public Radio (VCPR), complete with outrageous caller rants, satirical political podcasts, and hilarious commercials poking fun at modern social media culture.

### Custom In-Game Streaming / Spotify Integration

With modern car technology leaning heavily on Apple CarPlay and Spotify, one of the biggest wishlist features is seamless integration with personal streaming services or a custom "on-demand" playlist menu, allowing players to curate their own driving soundtracks.

## Conclusion

The GTA 6 soundtrack is shaping up to be Rockstar’s most immersive audio experience yet. By blending nostalgic 80s synth-pop, authentic Florida rap, vibrant Latin rhythms, and hilarious talk radio, the Vice City airwaves will perfectly capture the state's unique culture. Whether cruising down Ocean Drive or escaping the law, the game's radio stations will provide an unforgettable backdrop to every wild moment across Leonida.
