While the neon-drenched streets of Vice City are guaranteed to pull you in, the real magic of Grand Theft Auto VI lies far beyond the city limits. Rockstar Games has crafted the state of Leonida into an expansive, varied open world packed with sprawling wetlands, coastal keys, and forgotten rural backwaters.

If you're ready to steal a fast ride, hit the highway, and explore the wild side of Leonida, here are the absolute best places to visit outside Vice City.

## The Leonida Grassrivers (The Swampland)

Nothing captures the wild, unpredictable nature of Leonida quite like its vast central marshlands. Deep in the Grassrivers, the urban polish of Vice City gives way to mud, murky waterways, and aggressive local wildlife. Exploring this zone requires heavy vehicles, airboats, and a constant eye on your surroundings to survive the unpredictable environment.

### What to Expect?

Navigate endless airboat routes, dense mangrove forests, and treacherous murky waters. Keep your guard up for aggressive alligators hiding in the brush, as well as unpredictable, eccentric local NPCs.

### Why Visit?

This region serves as prime territory for off-road chaos, tracking down hidden off-the-grid hideouts, and hunting wild game. It provides a thrilling escape when you need a break from high-tech city life.

## The Leonida Keys & Highway Bridges

Stretching south from the mainland, Leonida's tropical island chain connected by massive highway bridges offers scenic driving routes across open ocean waters. It provides a sun-soaked getaway full of vibrant coastal life, high-speed boating opportunities, and relaxed seaside communities that contrast sharply with the crowded mainland streets.

### What to Expect?

Discover crystal-clear coastal waters, luxury resort towns, bustling marina docks, and sun-soaked beaches. You will also encounter sprawling highways suspended directly over the turquoise ocean waters.

### Why Visit?

Whether you want to test the absolute top speed of a supercar along endless ocean bridges or grab a high-powered boat to explore isolated barrier islands, the Keys deliver a coastal paradise.

## Ambrosia & Rural Backcountry

Moving inland brings you to Ambrosia and the surrounding agricultural heartland. This region serves as a stark contrast to Vice City's high-rise luxury, showcasing the rugged industrial, farming, and biker culture that keeps the state's backwoods economy running far away from tourist eyes.

### What to Expect?

Traverse winding dirt trails, truck stops, local watering holes, and industrial yards. The area features massive sugar refineries, farm fields, and industrial infrastructure tucked away near dense pine forests.

### Why Visit?

Ambrosia is perfect for low-profile criminal operations, dirt bike racing, and encountering oddball side quests. It offers plenty of room to cause mayhem far away from heavy urban police coverage.

## Port Gellhorn & Faded Coastal Towns

Dotted along the western coast away from the main metropolis are faded coastal towns like Port Gellhorn. These lower-income hubs bring a gritty, authentic atmosphere complete with local strip malls, cheap motels, dive bars, and active industrial shipping yards.

### What to Expect?

Expect run-down seaside motels, neon-lit convenience stores, local strip clubs, and working industrial docks. The area captures the vibe of a forgotten, down-and-dirty coastal town full of desperate locals.

### Why Visit?

Port Gellhorn is a hotspot for low-level robberies, street-level criminal activity, and low-key safehouses. It offers an ideal getaway spot when the heat in Vice City gets too intense.

## Mount Kalaga National Park

Elevated terrain and vast national parklands offer a rare change of altitude in Leonida's landscape. Cresting high forested ridgelines gives you panoramic views of the entire state stretching out toward the horizon, making it an essential destination for outdoor survivalists and explorers alike.

### What to Expect?

Discover winding mountain dirt roads, hidden hiking trails, dense forest tree cover, and riverways. High-elevation scenic lookouts offer sweeping views across Leonida's flat coastal planes down below.

### Why Visit?

High altitude spots are ideal for setting up getaway base camps, parachuting off peaks, and catching cinematic views. It is also the premier location on the map for hunting, fishing, and off-road driving.

## Tips for Exploring Leonida's Wilderness

Before venturing outside Vice City, pick the right vehicle like a lifted truck or airboat for swampy terrain. Watch out for sharp country sheriffs in rural areas. Finally, scan remote roads for dynamic encounters, hidden cults, and off-the-grid stash houses hidden throughout Leonida's vast wilderness.

### Pick the Right Vehicle

Standard sports cars will bog down instantly in the mud of the Grassrivers or the rough dirt trails of Mount Kalaga. Before heading into the backcountry, grab a lifted pickup truck, an off-road bike, or an airboat. Choosing terrain-appropriate rides ensures you can navigate deep swamps, scale steep inclines, and execute quick getaways without getting stranded far from civilization.

### Watch the Law

While rural Leonida has fewer police patrols than downtown Vice City, country sheriffs operate by their own rules. They are aggressive, know the terrain well, and often patrol remote highways and backcountry roads. Laying low outside the city can clear your wanted level, but causing trouble in small towns will quickly bring stubborn local law enforcement right to your door.

### Scan for Random Events

Leonida's wilderness is packed with dynamic open-world encounters hidden off the main paved highways. Keep your eyes peeled while traveling through rural zones to discover roadside breakdown traps, bizarre local cult gatherings, drug smuggling runs, and hidden stash houses. Investigating these random occurrences awards rare loot, extra cash, unique encounters, and valuable side activities away from the main story line.

## Final Thoughts

Vice City's neon skyline is impressive, but Leonida's true scale lies in its wild, unpredictable countryside. From murky swamps to scenic coastal highways, venturing off the beaten path yields rich rewards, hidden encounters, and unmatched open-world freedom. Gear up, pick your vehicle, and start exploring everything beyond the city limits.
