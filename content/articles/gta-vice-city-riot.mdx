The “Riot” mission in GTA Vice City is one of the first story missions that introduces Tommy Vercetti to the city's criminal business world. It involves helping Ken Rosenberg deal with a group of workers before the situation gets out of control.

Unlike some missions that focus on driving or shooting, Riot requires you to create enough chaos while following a specific objective. Knowing where to go, what to do, and how to avoid failing the mission makes it much easier to complete.

This blog explains how to start the Riot mission, what objectives you need to complete, and how to finish it successfully.

## What Is the Riot Mission in GTA Vice City?

“Riot” is a mission given by Avery Carrington, the real-estate developer Tommy meets early in the game. Avery asks Tommy to help him deal with striking workers who are causing problems at a construction site.

The mission takes place around a group of workers protesting near a construction area. Tommy must disguise himself, start a fight, and cause enough chaos to complete Avery's objective.

The mission also introduces a simple but important GTA Vice City mechanic: changing your appearance can help you enter areas or interact with groups without immediately attracting attention.

## How to Start the “Riot” Mission?

To start the Riot mission, you first need to progress through the early story missions until Avery Carrington becomes available.

Look for Avery's construction site in Washington Beach. His missions are marked with an “A” on the map.

Walk into the mission marker near Avery to trigger the cutscene. Avery explains the situation and asks Tommy to cause trouble among the protesting workers.

After the conversation ends, the mission officially begins.

## Riot Mission Objectives

Your main objectives are:

- Change into the required outfit.
- Travel to the protest area.
- Start a fight with the workers.
- Cause enough chaos to trigger the required reaction.
- Leave the area safely.

The game gives you a straightforward goal, but the mission can become difficult if you attack too aggressively or attract too much police attention.

## Step 1: Get the Right Outfit

After starting the mission, you need to change Tommy's clothing before going to the protest.

Follow the mission instructions and go to the designated clothing location. Once Tommy changes into the required outfit, you can continue toward the workers.

Changing clothes is important because it helps Tommy blend into the situation and allows the mission to progress properly.

Don't rush directly to the protest before completing this step.

## Step 2: Go to the Workers

Once you have the correct outfit, head toward the protest location.

You can use a nearby vehicle to reach the area quickly. Be careful while driving because getting into unnecessary accidents can attract police attention.

When you arrive, get out of the vehicle and approach the workers.

The objective is not simply to eliminate everyone. You need to create a riot and cause enough disruption for the mission to progress.

## Step 3: Start the Riot

This is the main part of the mission.

Begin attacking the workers to provoke them. You can use your fists or a suitable weapon, depending on your approach.

The important thing is to create enough chaos without getting overwhelmed.

Try to focus on a few workers at a time rather than running directly into the middle of the crowd. Move around when necessary and avoid staying surrounded.

The workers will begin fighting back, so keep an eye on Tommy's health.

## Step 4: Destroy the Required Vehicles

As the situation develops, you may need to destroy vehicles connected to the protest.

Use an available weapon or other method to damage the required vehicles. Keep some distance when dealing with explosions because Tommy can take significant damage.

If you use explosives, position yourself safely before detonating them.

The key is to complete the objective without accidentally killing Tommy or triggering unnecessary police attention.

## Step 5: Deal With the Police

The riot can attract police attention, especially after the situation becomes violent.

If you receive a wanted level, don't waste time fighting every police officer. Move away from the area and use streets or alleys to escape.

A quick getaway vehicle can make this much easier.

If your wanted level becomes difficult to manage, look for a Pay 'n' Spray to remove it when the mission allows it.

Avoid unnecessary confrontations. The mission is about completing Avery's objective, not fighting the entire police force.

## Step 6: Complete the Mission

Once you have fulfilled the required objectives, the mission will end.

You will receive a cash reward, and the story will continue with additional missions becoming available.

If the mission fails, don't worry. You can return to Avery's location and try again.

## Tips for Completing Riot Easily

### Use a Reliable Vehicle

Take a fast and durable vehicle before heading to the protest. A good vehicle gives you an easier escape if the police become involved.

### Don't Get Surrounded

The workers can quickly surround Tommy. Keep moving and create space whenever several enemies approach at once.

### Watch Your Health

Fighting several enemies at close range can drain Tommy's health quickly. Don't stay in one place for too long.

### Avoid Unnecessary Police Attention

The mission already has enough chaos. Don't make things harder by attacking police officers unless necessary.

### Use Weapons Carefully

Weapons can make the mission faster, but explosions can also hurt Tommy. Maintain a safe distance when using explosive weapons.

### Know the Area

Learning the streets around Washington Beach can help you escape quickly if the situation becomes dangerous.

## Common Reasons Players Fail the Riot Mission

The mission is relatively short, but a few mistakes can cause problems.

- **Going to the protest without changing clothes:** Follow the mission instructions and get the required outfit first.
- **Getting surrounded by workers:** Keep moving and avoid standing in the middle of the crowd.
- **Losing too much health:** Don't fight every enemy at close range. Create distance when Tommy's health gets low.
- **Attracting excessive police attention:** Complete the required objectives instead of causing additional destruction throughout the neighborhood.
- **Destroying the wrong vehicles:** Pay attention to the mission objective and make sure you are targeting the required vehicles.

## Is Riot Difficult in GTA Vice City?

The Riot mission in GTA Vice City is not among the game's hardest missions. Its objectives are simple, and the mission doesn't require complicated driving or precise shooting.

The biggest challenge is managing the crowd while keeping Tommy alive.

If you stay mobile, follow the objectives, and avoid unnecessary fights, you should be able to complete the mission without many problems.

## What Comes After the Riot Mission?

Completing Avery Carrington's missions helps Tommy build his relationship with the powerful real-estate developer.

As you progress through the game, more missions and opportunities become available. Avery's missions are also part of Tommy's wider journey as he establishes himself in Vice City.

The Riot mission is therefore more than a simple street fight. It is another step in Tommy's rise through Vice City's criminal world.

## Final Thoughts

The GTA Vice City “Riot” mission is a straightforward mission once you understand its objectives. Start it by visiting Avery Carrington at his construction site, follow the instructions to change Tommy's outfit, and then head to the workers' protest.

Create the required chaos, handle the objectives carefully, and leave the area when necessary. Keeping Tommy mobile and avoiding unnecessary police attention will make the mission much easier.

With the right approach, you can complete Riot in GTA Vice City quickly and continue Tommy Vercetti's story.
