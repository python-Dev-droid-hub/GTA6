Gone are the days of casually pulling a minigun, a rocket launcher, and three assault rifles out of thin air. With Grand Theft Auto VI shifting toward a much more grounded and tactical approach in the fictional state of Leonida, the way you manage, carry, and use your hardware has completely changed.

If you are stepping into Vice City for the first time or transitioning over from GTA Online, understanding how the new weapon ecosystem works will save you from getting gunned down by local law enforcement.

## The New Inventory Rules: Say Goodbye to the Magic Pocket

The biggest shock for returning players is the carry limit. Heavily inspired by Red Dead Redemption 2, GTA 6 implements a restricted inventory system.

### The Carry Cap

You can no longer hoard every weapon you pick up. Protagonists Jason and Lucia are limited to carrying a compact selection on their person: typically one handgun, a couple of primary long guns slung over the shoulder or back, and a handful of throwables.

### Vehicle Trunks as Armories

Your personal car or truck is now your mobile base of operations. If you need to swap out a standard tactical carbine for a heavy machine gun or a shotgun, you must access your vehicle's trunk. Planning your loadout before a heist or a major shootout is mandatory.

## Confirmed Weapon Classes and What to Look For

Rockstar has loaded the game with over 30 unique, highly detailed firearms spanning classic archetypes and real-world inspirations.

| Weapon Class | Key Examples | Tactical Advantage |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Handguns | Polymer Pistol (Glock-style), Heavy Pistol (M1911), Mustang .357 | Easily concealed; essential for quick-draw scenarios and keeping a low profile around police without instantly triggering a wanted level. |
| Assault Rifles | Carbine Rifle (AR-15/HK416 style), AK-47 variants | The absolute workhorses for street firefights, offering high stopping power and modular versatility. |
| Shotguns | Pump-Action (Mossberg 590), Double-Barreled Shotgun | Devastating at close range; ideal for clearing tight interior spaces during store robberies or home invasions. |
| SMGs | MP5-inspired tactical SMG, Micro Uzi, Compact SMG | High fire rates designed for drive-bys, motorcycle chases, and chaotic corridor clears. |
| Precision | Bolt-Action Sniper (Remington 700), Assault Sniper | Long-range threat neutralization and vehicle blockades. |
| Heavy & Specialty | RPG-7, Milkor MGL Grenade Launcher, Speargun | Vehicle-killers for armored opposition; the speargun introduces dedicated underwater combat for aquatic exploration. |

## Deep Customization and Tactical Gear

Weapons in GTA 6 aren't just picked up and used as-is; they feature granular customization options at local gun shops and workbenches.

### Workbench Upgrades

You can alter your gear with optical scopes, extended magazines, suppressors, custom grips, and unique skin finishes to match your playstyle.

### Tactical Equipment Integration

Beyond firearms, the weapon wheel shares space with utility tools. Flashbangs, smoke grenades, lockpicks, zip ties, and a dedicated taser allow for stealthier, more methodical approaches to missions rather than just running in guns blazing.

## Conclusion

Mastering the revised weapon ecosystem in Grand Theft Auto VI requires a major shift from mindless hoarding to tactical preparation. By respecting the new carry limits, utilizing vehicle trunks as mobile armories, and carefully curating your loadout for every job, you will survive the chaotic streets of Leonida. Gear up wisely, keep your heavy iron secure, and always plan your exit strategy before pulling the trigger.
