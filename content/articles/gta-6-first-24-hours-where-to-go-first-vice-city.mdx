The long wait for Grand Theft Auto VI is officially reaching its climax. As players across the world gear up to jump into the sprawling, sun-drenched state of Leonida, one giant question hangs over launch day: where do you go first?

With modern Vice City serving as the beating heart of the most detailed open world Rockstar Games has ever constructed, blindly wandering around can quickly lead to decision paralysis. Whether you want to soak in the neon aesthetics, experiment with brand-new gameplay mechanics, or just test the limits of the physics engine, here is your definitive first-day exploration guide for your first 24 hours in Vice City.

## Ocean Drive & Vice Beach: The Nostalgia Warm-Up

Kick off your Leonida adventure right where Vice City's legacy began. Cruising down the vibrant, sun-drenched boulevard of Ocean Drive offers an instant hit of classic GTA nostalgia. With pastel neon architecture, swaying palm trees, and ocean views, this iconic coastline serves as the ideal starting ground to soak in the atmosphere before diving deep into heavy criminal operations.

### Why Visit First?

Visiting Vice Beach first gives you a clear visual benchmark for how far the franchise has evolved. Stepping onto the sand allows you to immediately appreciate the massive leap in graphical fidelity compared to earlier titles. Beyond aesthetics, it provides a safe, wide-open environment to master basic character movement, vehicle handling, and camera controls without immediate pressure from heavy police pursuit.

### What to Look for?

Keep an eye on the hyper-realistic ocean water rendering and volumetric lighting bouncing off oceanfront buildings. Pay close attention to crowd dynamics—NPCs actively sunbathe, record videos on their phones, and jog with lifelike pathfinding. You will also notice seamless transitions between outdoor street stalls, beachfront cafes, and open coastal boardwalks that bring this legendary seaside strip to life.

## Downtown Vice City: Test the Verticality & Interior Physics

Head inland to the bustling commercial hub of Downtown Vice City to experience a dense, towering urban core. Unlike past open-world maps that relied mostly on static building facades, Downtown showcases incredible vertical scale, bustling multi-level plazas, and complex structural architecture. It serves as the ultimate playground for testing how GTA 6 handles dense urban navigation and realistic structural interactions.

### Why Visit First?

Downtown is the best location to test Rockstar's upgraded interior mechanics and collision physics early in your playthrough. Cruising through tight city corridors and entering accessible skyscrapers allows you to evaluate indoor gunplay mechanics, stealth pathfinding, and character movement dynamics inside enclosed spaces before diving into complex story heist missions that demand fast, precise movement under high pressure.

### What to Look for?

Keep an eye out for seamlessly accessible hotel lobbies, bank foyers, and multi-story parking garages without loading screens. Pay close attention to environmental destruction mechanics—such as shattering glass, bullet-riddled drywall, and reactive furniture. Additionally, observe how the dynamic lighting engine realistically shifts as you move from bright exterior street level into dimly lit subterranean alleys and interior hallways.

## Grassrivers (The Everglades): Airboats, Wildlife, & Mud Mechanics

Leave the neon skyline behind and pilot an airboat into Grassrivers, Leonida's vast, murky wetland preserve. This expansive swamp region trades paved streets for winding waterways, dense sawgrass, and treacherous terrain. It is designed to challenge your survival skills, test off-road navigation, and push the game's custom environmental physics engine to its absolute limits in a untamed setting.

### Why Visit First?

Exploring Grassrivers early highlights the staggering diversity of Leonida's map beyond Vice City's urban borders. It offers an immediate taste of wilderness mechanics, unique off-road vehicle handling, and aquatic physics. Visiting early allows you to practice maneuvering unpredictable terrain, master airboat controls, and learn how to handle aggressive animal encounters before high-stakes storyline missions force you into swamp territory.

### What to Look for?

Observe how mud, water, and thick sludge realistically affect your vehicle's tire traction, speed, and body paint. Pay close attention to dynamic wildlife behaviors, such as territorial alligators hunting prey along murky riverbanks. Additionally, watch how airboat propellers create realistic water wake patterns and kick up floating debris, showcasing Rockstar's advanced fluid simulation technology in real-time.

## The Leonida Keys: Long Drives & Highway Physics

Stretch your wheels across the massive overseas highway system connecting the mainland to the tropical island chain of the Leonida Keys. Flanked by endless ocean on both sides, this scenic interstate corridor offers uninterrupted stretches of asphalt, ideal for opening up throttle, cruising at high speeds, and soaking in the sun-drenched coastal vistas defining southern Leonida's laid-back island vibe.

### Why Visit First?

Taking a long highway drive early on gives you the perfect opportunity to master high-speed vehicle physics and cornering controls without navigating tight city corners. It lets you test top acceleration, weight distribution during lane changes, and high-speed braking dynamics across various vehicle classes, ensuring you are fully prepared for intense, fast-paced highway police pursuits later in the campaign.

### What to Look for?

Notice how wind drag, tire grip, and road elevation changes subtly affect top speeds and vehicle stability. Listen closely to spatial audio cues like roaring engines, rushing coastal crosswinds, and radio station chatter blending into ambient ocean sounds. Additionally, observe the seamless draw distances, shimmering asphalt heat haze effects, and dynamic horizon rendering across the sprawling ocean bridges.

## Nightlife & Modern Social Media: The E11EVEN Nod & In-Game Phone

Experience Vice City's electrifying nocturnal culture, where neon-lit streets lead to high-end ultraclubs inspired by famous real-world venues like E11EVEN. Here, nightlife merges seamlessly with an updated in-game smartphone interface. Players can record chaotic city moments, stream live events, scroll parody social feeds, and engage with a hyper-connected digital landscape that perfectly mirrors contemporary internet and celebrity culture.

### Why Visit First?

Diving into the nightlife scene early reveals how social media mechanics directly influence open-world gameplay. It gives you a firsthand look at how public actions trigger viral reactions, dynamic news reports, and NPC interactions. Exploring clubs on night one also lets you sample the soundtrack, test smartphone app features, and get comfortable managing digital reputation systems early in your playthrough.

### What to Look for?

Watch how ambient club lighting, laser displays, and heavy bass reverberate off interior crowds in real time. Pay close attention to how NPCs react when you pull out your smartphone camera to record them. Additionally, explore the in-game social media feeds for live updates, viral video clips of your recent antics, and dynamic trending hashtags posted by Vice City residents.

| Hours 5–8 | Downtown Vice City | Explore indoor environments, small-scale robberies, and NPC interactions. |
| Hours 9–14 | Grassrivers Wetlands | Test airboats, off-road mechanics, and wildlife reactions. |
| Hours 15–18 | The Leonida Keys | Test vehicle speeds, radio channels, and on-demand podcasts. |

## Final Thoughts

Vice City is shaping up to be Rockstar's most immersive open world yet. Whether you are soaking in the nostalgia along Ocean Drive, testing building interiors downtown, tackling swamp physics in Grassrivers, cruising the Keys, or diving into neon nightlife, your first 24 hours set the tone. Take your time, test every mechanic, and let Leonida unfold at your own pace—the ultimate crime saga has officially begun.
