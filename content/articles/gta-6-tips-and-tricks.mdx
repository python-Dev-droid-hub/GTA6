Step into the neon-soaked streets of Leonida because Rockstar’s next masterpiece is almost here. Whether you are a veteran of Los Santos or diving into a modern Rockstar title for the first time, surviving the fictionalized version of Miami requires a shift in strategy. The mechanics are deeper, the cops are smarter, and the world reacts to you in ways the series has never seen before.

Keep these 15 essential tips and tricks in mind to get ahead from day one.

## 1. Master the New Vehicle and Trunk Weapon Limits

Gone are the days of carrying an entire military arsenal in your back pocket. GTA 6 adopts a realistic weapon carriage system: you can only carry two small firearms and one long rifle on your body. Heavy weapons like sniper rifles and rocket launchers must be stored in your vehicle's trunk. Always plan your loadout before stepping out of your car, or you might find yourself outgunned.

## 2. Pay Attention to the NPC Memory System

Pedestrians and local vendors in Leonida have long memories. If you cause trouble, rob a store, or pick a fight in a specific neighborhood, civilians will recognize your face or clothing on your return and flag down regional authorities. Change your outfits frequently to stay under the radar.

## 3. Respect the Law Enforcement Bodycam Era

Escaping a wanted level is no longer just about hiding out of sight until the minimap flashes. Police units track details dynamically via bodycams and cruiser dash cams, logging your vehicle's make, color, and license plate. To truly shake a high-heat pursuit, you need to swap vehicles or repaint your ride out of line-of-sight.

## 4. Prepare for the Return of the 6-Star Wanted Level

When things go completely sideways, the maximum threat level is back with a vengeance. Expect militarized roadblocks, tactical vehicle maneuvers, and advanced helicopter tracking. Surviving a 6-star level requires utilizing the terrain and bypassing major highway checkpoints.

## 5. Leverage the Dual-Protagonist Trust System

Jason and Lucia’s dynamic shapes both the narrative and moment-to-moment gameplay. Completing co-op open-world activities, exploring Leonida together, and sharing loot builds their mutual trust. High trust unlocks synchronized combat moves and getaway perks, whereas a strained relationship can make them hesitant to share ammunition under fire.

## 6. Use the In-Game Smartphone for Live Social Feeds

Don't just ignore your phone calls and texts. GTA 6's smartphone features live social media feeds and local news bulletins that dynamically track street races, open-air markets, and active police standoffs. Checking these feeds regularly is the fastest way to discover high-payout robberies and hidden map secrets.

## 7. Utilize Suppression Fire and Non-Lethal Tactics

You don’t always have to go in guns blazing. Firing close to targets—known as suppression fire—disorients and frightens civilians or lower-level threats, allowing you to clear rooms or force non-lethal surrenders without triggering massive police responses.

## 8. Calibrate Your Camera Deadzones Early

Before diving into the prologue, head into the settings menu to configure your visual layout (targeting a stable performance mode if on console) and fine-tune your controller deadzones. The game's refined shooting mechanics demand precise aiming, and dialing in your stick sensitivity early prevents accidental analog drift.

## 9. Explore Fully Accessible Interiors

Vice City and the surrounding regions feature hundreds of enterable, highly detailed interior locations, including fully explorable malls, nightclubs, motels, and convenience stores. Take time to explore these spaces—they often house unique loot, side interactions, and safe hiding spots during chases.

## 10. Manage Character Fitness and Health Items

Just like in past entries, physical upkeep matters. Visiting local gyms increases strength, while overeating changes body composition. Additionally, medical items like Zombix provide necessary health boosts, but relying on them too frequently diminishes their effectiveness over time.

## 11. Master Car-Chase Seat Switching

When cruising with your partner, you aren't locked into a single role. During a high-speed pursuit, you can switch seats to let the AI drive while you lean out the window to handle incoming fire, or vice versa. Use this mechanic to effectively manage intense firefights on the move.

## 12. Check Out the RDR2-Inspired Interaction Wheel

Interacting with the world feels more grounded thanks to an evolved conversation and interaction system. When your weapon is holstered, you can target NPCs to greet, provoke, or defuse situations. Pay attention to your surroundings, though—NPCs will notice if you stand too close or look like you are following them.

## 13. Keep an Eye Out for Dynamic Random Events

Leonida is packed with spontaneous encounters. Whether it's a roadside breakdown, a strange character sitting in a dumpster offering cryptic advice, or local wildlife roaming the swamps, interacting with these random events yields unique rewards, cash, and lore.

## 14. Fast Travel via the Ryde Me App

The map is massive, stretching from urban neon districts down to the sprawling Leonida Keys and wild marshlands. When you don't feel like driving across the entire state, pull up your phone and use the in-game taxi app, Ryde Me, to fast-track your journey across long distances.

## 15. Don't Skip the Side Activities and Wildlife Tracking

Beyond the criminal underworld, Leonida offers a massive slate of open-world diversions, including mini-golf, scuba diving, kayaking, and cataloging animals in the wild or at the local zoo. Taking a break from the hustle to engage with these side activities makes the world feel infinitely more alive.

## Final Thoughts

Surviving the expansive wilds and neon-lit streets of Leonida requires more than just raw shooting skills; it demands adaptability, situational awareness, and respect for the game’s deeply simulated mechanics. By mastering vehicle limits, managing your heat with law enforcement, and utilizing the dual-protagonist bond between Jason and Lucia, you will stay a step ahead of the competition. Dive in, explore every corner of Vice City, and carve your own legacy in Rockstar's newest criminal underworld.
