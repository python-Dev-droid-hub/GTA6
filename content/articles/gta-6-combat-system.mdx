If you walk into [Grand Theft Auto VI](/) expecting the run-and-gun arcade chaos of GTA V, you will hit the pavement fast. Rockstar has overhauled its combat engine, blending the grounded, weightier gunplay of Red Dead Redemption 2 with tactical close-quarters mechanics designed around dual protagonists Jason and Lucia.

From Focus mode and anatomical target overlays to destructible environments and weapon concealment, every shootout in Leonida rewards preparation and positioning over mindless spraying. Here is everything you need to know about how the GTA 6 combat system works—and how to survive every encounter.

## How Gunplay Has Changed in GTA 6?

Rockstar replaced GTA V’s simple dot crosshair with a dynamic aiming reticle and an upgraded ballistics model. Weapons carry real weight, recoil demands burst control, and how you carry your arsenal matters before a single shot is fired.

### Two-Weapon Quick Swap & Trunk Storage

You no longer pull a rocket launcher out of thin air. Jason and Lucia can quick-swap between two equipped firearms without opening the weapon wheel, while heavier rifles and specialized gear are stored in your vehicle’s trunk.

### Weapon Concealment

Walking down a Vice City sidewalk with a gun drawn triggers immediate NPC panic and police calls. Protagonists can hold smaller firearms behind their backs or conceal them under clothing until the exact moment you initiate a holdup or ambush.

### Physical Loot Restrictions

Carrying heavy duffel bags of cash, drugs, or stolen goods physically restricts your movement and limits you to one-handed firearms (like pistols or sawed-off shotguns) until you drop the bag or stash it in a getaway vehicle.

## Mastering Focus Mode & Lethal vs. Non-Lethal Targeting

One of the biggest additions to GTA 6’s gunfights is Focus (activated by clicking both analog sticks). While its primary out-of-combat use highlights lootable items and tracks environmental audio cues, activating Focus during a shootout slows down time and reveals an anatomical targeting overlay:

| Marker Color | Target Zone | Tactical Effect | Best Used For |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Red Markers | Head, heart, upper torso | Instant or rapid lethal takedowns | Heavily armed cartel members, SWAT, high-threat bosses |
| Yellow Markers | Knees, calves, hands, shoulders | Non-lethal disarming or incapacitation | Store clerks, armed civilians, keeping your Criminal Profile clean |

### Why Non-Lethal Shots Matter?

In GTA 6, every kill and crime feeds into Jason and Lucia’s Criminal Profile (ranging from Professional to Irredeemable). Shooting an armed gas station clerk in the hand or leg neutralizes the threat without escalating your profile into reckless territory—or drawing maximum police heat.

To permanently extend your Focus meter, spend time outside of missions at shooting ranges, golf putting greens, pool tables, and fishing spots.

## Cover Mechanics & Destructible Environments

Taking cover is no longer a guarantee of invulnerability. GTA 6 introduces destructible walls and thin cover during indoor and urban firefights.

### Soft Cover vs. Hard Cover

Drywall, wooden doors, plaster partitions, and car doors can be shredded by sustained automatic fire or shotgun blasts. Always prioritize concrete pillars, brick walls, engine blocks, and heavy steel fixtures when pinned down.

### Dynamic Hit Reactions & Downed Enemies

Enemies don't simply drop dead when their health bar hits zero. NPCs suffer localized limb injuries, limp, crawl behind cover, and can even continue firing from the ground if not properly neutralized. Watch downed enemies closely before pushing forward.

### Fire & Environmental Hazards

Molotovs and explosive rounds spread flames realistically across dry brush, wooden interiors, and leaking fuel trails—use fire to flush entrenched enemies out of tight rooms.

## Dual-Protagonist Tactics: Hot-Swapping Jason & Lucia

Unlike GTA V’s wide aerial camera zoom when switching characters, GTA 6 lets you hot-swap between Jason and Lucia almost instantaneously during joint combat encounters. Each protagonist brings distinct strengths to a firefight:

### Jason (Ex-Military Precision)

Excels at tactical positioning, long-range marksmanship, and suppressed engagements. Use Jason to hold angles, pick off distant shooters, and control choke points.

### Lucia (Close-Quarters Brawler)

Trained in hand-to-hand combat by her father, Lucia shines in tight interiors, quicktime melee counters, and aggressive flanking.

### How to Use Hot-Swapping in Combat?

Pin an enemy squad down with suppressing fire using Jason, then immediately hot-swap to Lucia to flank their blind spot while their heads are down. Because your partner’s AI continues laying down cover fire while you move, crossfire setups become your strongest tool in large-scale ambushes.

## Melee Combat, Stamina & Physical Conditioning

Close-quarters combat has been rebuilt from the ground up with dedicated grappling, counters, bladed weapons, and contextual quicktime events. However, button-mashing will leave you exhausted and defenseless.

### Manage Your Stamina Bar

Sprinting, dodging, and throwing heavy melee strikes drain stamina rapidly. Back off briefly between combos to let your stamina recover.

### Train at the Gym

Your physical conditioning directly impacts combat survivability. Strength training (like bench pressing) permanently increases your maximum health bar, while cardio workouts expand your stamina pool. Conversely, overeating junk food and smoking without working out can weigh your character down and hurt mobility.

## Surviving Enemy Encounters & The 6-Star Wanted System

Winning the gunfight is only half the battle—escaping the aftermath requires understanding how witnesses and law enforcement operate in Leonida.

### Watch for Armed Civilians

Not every bystander will run away screaming. Robbing small businesses or starting street brawls can prompt armed locals to draw weapons or attack you with makeshift tools.

### Eliminate Witnesses Quietly

Police do not teleport to your location automatically. A crime is only reported if an NPC witnesses it or an alarm is triggered. Use suppressed weapons or melee takedowns to keep stealth raids quiet.

### Break Line of Sight & Change Your Description

When the six-star wanted system activates, police track four specific identifiers: your face, your outfit, your vehicle, and whether you are solo or paired up. Once your wanted stars turn from white to red (indicating active search mode rather than direct pursuit), duck into an alley, swap hats/masks/glasses from your item wheel, and switch to a clean vehicle to slip right past patrol cars.

## Conclusion

Mastering the GTA 6 combat system comes down to preparation, positioning, and restraint. Pack your trunk before missions, stick to solid cover, and use Focus mode to choose between lethal force and non-lethal incapacitation. Pair Jason and Lucia’s distinct strengths in crossfires, and you will survive every shootout in Leonida.
