The “The Job” mission in GTA Vice City is one of the most memorable heist missions in the game. Tommy Vercetti and his crew plan to rob the El Banco Corrupto Grande in Little Havana. The mission combines disguises, a bank robbery, hostage control, a shootout, and a difficult escape.

If you want to complete the bank robbery without getting stuck, you need to follow the plan carefully and react quickly when the police arrive. This blog explains how to start and complete “The Job” mission, including useful tips for each stage.

## How to Start “The Job” Mission in GTA Vice City?

To unlock “The Job,” you first need to purchase Print Works and complete the required asset missions connected to your businesses.

Once Print Works is producing income, you can receive the mission from Ken Rosenberg. The mission takes place in Little Havana, where the crew prepares to rob the bank.

Before starting, make sure Tommy has plenty of health and armor. Bringing a reliable weapon can also make the escape much easier.

## Meet the Crew at Print Works

The mission begins when Tommy meets the members of his robbery crew at Print Works.

The crew includes:

- Tommy Vercetti
- Phil Cassidy
- Hilary King
- Cam Jones

Each member has a specific role during the robbery. After the group finishes preparing, everyone heads toward the bank.

The mission then moves into the robbery phase.

## Enter the Bank and Start the Robbery

Drive to El Banco Corrupto Grande in Little Havana with the crew.

When you arrive, enter the bank and follow your teammates. Tommy and the crew begin the robbery while Hilary waits outside with the getaway car.

Inside the bank, the first objective is to reach the security area and deal with the guards.

Keep moving through the building and follow the mission markers. Avoid wasting ammunition because you will need plenty of firepower during the escape.

## Get the Bank Manager

One important part of the mission is forcing the bank manager to cooperate.

Move through the bank and reach the manager's area. Tommy needs to get the manager to open the vault so the crew can take the money.

The situation becomes more complicated when the manager refuses to cooperate.

Move carefully and follow the objective until the vault can be accessed.

## Open the Vault

Once the manager is dealt with, the crew can access the vault.

Enter the vault area and collect the money as instructed. At this point, the robbery appears to be going according to plan.

However, the situation quickly changes when the police become involved.

The alarm brings law enforcement to the bank, turning the planned robbery into a major firefight.

## Fight Your Way Out of the Bank

After the alarm is triggered, police officers begin entering the building.

Use cover whenever possible and eliminate the officers blocking your path. Keep an eye on your health because several enemies can attack from different directions.

Phil and Cam help Tommy during the fight, but you still need to handle enemies quickly.

Once the route is clear, move toward the exit and prepare for an even tougher battle outside.

## Escape From the Bank

Getting out of the bank is one of the hardest parts of “The Job.”

When Tommy and the crew leave the building, police officers surround the area. Hilary also becomes involved in the escape.

Stay focused on the enemies directly blocking your route instead of chasing every police officer.

Use powerful weapons and keep moving. Standing in one position for too long can allow police officers to surround Tommy.

## Protect the Crew During the Escape

The mission becomes more challenging because the crew members are vulnerable during the escape.

Try to clear enemies around your teammates and create a path toward the getaway vehicle.

Hilary's role is especially important because he drives the escape vehicle. Unfortunately, he can be killed during the shootout, making the escape much more difficult.

If you want to make the mission easier, eliminate nearby police officers quickly and avoid unnecessary movement.

## Get to the Getaway Vehicle

After fighting your way out, head toward the getaway vehicle.

Follow the objective marker and stay close to the crew. Once you reach the vehicle, get inside and prepare to escape the police.

The wanted level makes this part difficult, so don't waste time fighting every police officer.

Your main goal is to survive and get away from the area.

## How to Lose the Police?

Once you're away from the bank, you need to escape the police and lose your wanted level.

Avoid unnecessary crashes while driving because damaging the vehicle can make the escape harder.

Use side streets and alleys to break away from pursuing police vehicles. If you have access to a Pay 'n' Spray, you can use it to remove your wanted level when the mission allows it.

Keep driving until the police are no longer an immediate threat.

## Tips to Complete “The Job” Mission

Here are some useful tips to make the bank robbery easier:

### Bring Armor

Armor gives Tommy extra protection during the bank shootout. Make sure you have armor before starting the mission.

### Carry Powerful Weapons

A shotgun, SMG, or other reliable weapon can help you eliminate police officers quickly.

### Don't Waste Ammunition

The mission contains several firefights, so conserve ammunition during the early sections.

### Protect Hilary

Hilary is important to the escape plan. Try to clear police officers around him before moving toward the getaway vehicle.

### Keep Moving

Standing still during the escape can leave Tommy surrounded. Move between cover and keep enemies under control.

### Watch Your Health

The police can deal significant damage during the final shootout. If your health gets low, prioritize survival instead of attacking every enemy.

## What Happens After “The Job”?

After Tommy and the crew escape, the bank robbery mission is completed. The successful heist helps Tommy's criminal operation generate more money and strengthens his control over Vice City.

“The Job” is also important because it demonstrates how Tommy's businesses and criminal empire are becoming increasingly organized.

The mission combines several different gameplay mechanics, making it more memorable than a simple driving or shooting objective.

## Final Thoughts

GTA Vice City’s “The Job” mission is a challenging bank robbery that requires preparation, accurate shooting, and a quick escape. Start with enough health, armor, and ammunition, then follow the crew through the bank and focus on surviving the police ambush.

The most difficult section is usually the escape, especially when trying to protect the crew. Keep moving, use cover, eliminate nearby enemies, and reach the getaway vehicle as quickly as possible.

Once you successfully lose the police, the bank robbery is complete and you can continue building Tommy Vercetti's empire across Vice City.
