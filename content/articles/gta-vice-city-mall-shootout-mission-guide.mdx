The “Mall Shootout” mission in GTA Vice City is one of the early missions that tests your shooting skills and ability to chase a target. Tommy Vercetti must meet a contact at the Washington Beach Mall, but the meeting quickly turns into a gunfight and pursuit.

In this blog, you will learn how to start the Mall Shootout mission, defeat the attackers, chase the target, and complete every objective without unnecessary trouble.

## How to Start the Mall Shootout Mission?

To begin the Mall Shootout mission, head to the mission marker at the Washington Beach Mall. Once you arrive, a short cutscene introduces the situation and sets up the encounter.

After the conversation, the mission immediately becomes a combat sequence. Make sure Tommy has a suitable weapon before starting. A pistol or another accurate firearm can make the first part of the mission easier.

## What Happens in the Mall Shootout Mission?

The meeting does not go as planned. After Tommy meets the contact, enemies appear and the situation turns hostile. Your objective is to eliminate the attackers and pursue the person who escapes.

The mission combines several simple tasks:

- Meet the contact at the mall.
- Survive the initial shootout.
- Eliminate the hostile enemies.
- Follow the escaping target.
- Chase the target through Vice City.
- Take down the target to finish the mission.

The key is to stay focused during the transition from the gunfight to the chase.

## How to Complete the Mall Shootout Mission?

### 1. Arrive at the Washington Beach Mall

Drive to the marked location and enter the mission area. Follow the marker until the cutscene begins.

You do not need to rush through this section. Park your vehicle in a convenient position so you can leave quickly if the mission turns into a chase.

### 2. Prepare for the Shootout

Once the cutscene ends, enemies will attack. Move into a safe position and avoid standing in the open.

Use nearby objects and walls for cover whenever possible. Instead of firing continuously, aim carefully and eliminate enemies one at a time.

### 3. Eliminate the Attackers

Focus on the enemies closest to Tommy first. Keep moving between shots so you are not an easy target.

If your health starts dropping quickly, retreat behind cover and wait for a safer opportunity to attack. Accurate shots are more useful than wasting ammunition.

### 4. Watch for the Escaping Target

After dealing with the attackers, the mission shifts from a shootout to a pursuit. The target will attempt to escape.

Get into a fast vehicle and follow the mission marker. Avoid unnecessary crashes because losing too much time can make the chase harder.

### 5. Chase the Target

Stay close to the target without driving recklessly. Vice City's traffic can make the pursuit difficult, especially when the target changes direction.

Use wider roads whenever possible and control your speed around corners. If you crash, recover quickly and continue following the target.

### 6. Take Down the Target

When you catch up, use your weapon or vehicle to stop the target. Once the target is eliminated and the mission objectives are completed, the mission will end.

You should then receive your mission reward and continue with the next available missions.

## Tips for Completing Mall Shootout

The mission becomes easier if you approach it carefully. Keep these tips in mind:

- **Bring a Reliable Weapon:** An accurate firearm makes the shootout easier.
- **Use Cover:** Avoid standing in the middle of open areas during combat.
- **Watch Your Health:** Do not continue attacking if Tommy is taking heavy damage.
- **Keep a Vehicle Nearby:** You may need it immediately after the shootout.
- **Avoid Unnecessary Traffic Collisions:** Crashes can slow you down during the chase.
- **Follow the Target Closely:** Taking random shortcuts can cause you to lose the mission target.
- **Stay Calm During Combat:** Controlled shooting is more effective than rapidly wasting ammunition.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

One common mistake is running directly toward the enemies. This leaves Tommy exposed and can cause his health to fall quickly.

Another mistake is ignoring your vehicle's position. If you leave your car too far away, you may waste valuable time getting back to it when the chase begins.

Players should also avoid getting distracted by pedestrians or police during the pursuit. The objective is to complete the mission, so focus on the target and follow the route.

## Is the Mall Shootout Mission Difficult?

The Mall Shootout mission is manageable once you understand its structure. The combat section can be challenging if you have limited health or ammunition, while the chase requires reasonable driving control.

The best strategy is simple: use cover during the shootout, eliminate enemies carefully, then get into a vehicle and follow the escaping target.

## Final Thoughts

The GTA Vice City “Mall Shootout” mission combines a short firefight with a vehicle pursuit. Start by reaching the Washington Beach Mall, prepare for the attack, eliminate the enemies, and then chase down the escaping target.

With a good weapon, careful positioning, and controlled driving, you can complete the mission without much difficulty. Once it is finished, you can move forward with Tommy Vercetti's growing criminal empire and the next missions in GTA Vice City.
