Rockstar Games has always treated vehicles as central to the [Grand Theft Auto](/) experience, and GTA 6 car customization pushes tuning, aesthetics, and utility further than ever before. Across the sun-soaked highways of Vice City and the muddy backroads of Leonida, modifying your ride goes far beyond slapping on a fresh coat of paint and bulletproofing the tires.

Drawing from confirmed gameplay details and recent footage breakdowns, here are the 10 essential GTA 6 car customization features every player should know before hitting the streets of Leonida.

## Specialized Mod Shops Instead of One-Size-Fits-All Garages

In previous entries, a single Los Santos Customs shop handled nearly every modification. In GTA 6, vehicle tuning is split across specialized in-game shops that each focus on distinct categories of upgrades. Expect dedicated garages for street performance, classic restorations, lowriders and donks (such as Rideout Customs), and basic quick-respray Pay 'n' Spray locations.

## Distinct Induction Systems: Turbochargers vs. Superchargers

Performance tuning now distinguishes between forced-induction setups rather than forcing a generic turbo onto every build. Mod menus feature separate options for stock setups, turbochargers, and superchargers—each altering power delivery and producing its own authentic engine note.

## Granular Engine & Stage Tuning

Under-the-hood customization borrows inspiration from dedicated racing titles. Players can browse part-by-part upgrade trees—including air intakes, air filters, and multi-tier Stage 1 through Stage 3 engine tunes—complete with detailed visual models inside the engine bay.

## Separate Asphalt and Off-Road Handling Stats

Tuning your suspension and tires directly impacts how your car behaves on different surfaces. Modification menus break down vehicle performance into distinct metrics, including:

- Top Speed & Acceleration
- Asphalt Handling (for street racing and city escapes)
- Off-Road Handling (for mudding, gravel, and Leonida's wetlands)
- Vehicle Strength (durability against heavy crash physics)

## Nitrous Oxide Upgrades

Street racers get a dedicated boost mechanic with installable nitrous systems. Pairing nitrous upgrades with street-racing builds gives players an extra edge during high-speed highway pulls and late-night illegal races.

## Deep Visual Customization and Elaborate Liveries

Aesthetic customization expands well beyond basic two-tone paint jobs. Players can apply intricate, full-body wraps—including detailed anime-inspired liveries—alongside custom bumpers, grilles, off-road light bars, and interior trim adjustments.

## Real-Time Stat Comparison Menus

Borrowing a quality-of-life feature from modern racers like Forza Horizon and Need for Speed, the GTA 6 car customization interface highlights live stat shifts as you scroll through parts. You can immediately see whether a heavier bumper or wider tire setup improves grip at the cost of acceleration before spending cash.

## Tracker Removal Through Chop-Shop Fences

Stealing a high-end exotic car off the street no longer means you can simply drive it into your personal garage and claim ownership. Luxury vehicles come equipped with anti-theft trackers that must be disabled through underground fences before you can customize or store the car as your own.

## Trunk Loadouts and Functional Vehicle Storage

Personalizing a vehicle isn't strictly mechanical—it is also tactical. Interacting with your car opens dedicated Loadout and Storage options, turning your trunk into a mobile stash for extra weapons, gear, and stolen loot during jobs.

## Expandable Garage Properties With Built-In Utility

Your personal fleet starts small, with limited initial storage slots that expand as you purchase garage properties across the map. High-end garages double as operational hubs, offering built-in weapon lockers and drop-off points for stolen goods alongside space to showcase your custom builds.

## Final Thoughts

With specialized workshops, surface-specific handling stats, and deeper mechanical tuning, GTA 6 car customization bridges the gap between open-world action and dedicated street-racing culture. Whether you are building a supercharged muscle car for Vice City boulevards or a lifted off-roader for Leonida's backwoods, every upgrade carries real weight on the road.
