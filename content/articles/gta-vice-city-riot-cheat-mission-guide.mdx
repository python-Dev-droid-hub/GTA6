Want to turn a quiet [Vice City](/) street into a brawl? On PC, type FIGHTFIGHTFIGHT during gameplay to activate the pedestrian riot cheat. If you’re stuck on the Riot story mission, the trick is to punch four different workers at the Spand Express depot. Security will open the gate, giving you access to the three delivery vans you need to destroy.

## How To Start a Pedestrian Riot in GTA Vice City?

Make a manual save before entering the cheat. Then head to a busy street in free roam so you can see the effect as soon as it kicks in. Pedestrians will start fighting each other, and some may come after Tommy too. If you want to see the chaos in another part of Vice City, drive to a different neighborhood and find another crowd.

| Platform And Edition | GTA Vice City Riot Cheat |
| --- | --- |
| PC, original and Definitive Edition | FIGHTFIGHTFIGHT |
| PlayStation, Definitive Edition | Down, Left, Up, Left, Cross (×), R2, R1, L2, L1 |

On PC, type the code as one word while the game is running. On PlayStation, press the buttons in the order shown. When you’re ready to go back to the story, reload the save you made before activating the cheat. Keep that save in its own slot, because the riot effect can stay active in a save made after using it. Cheats can also disable trophies or achievements in the Definitive Edition.

## How to Complete the GTA Vice City Riot Mission?

Riot unlocks after Jury Fury. Return to Ken Rosenberg’s office to start the mission. In the opening cutscene, property developer Avery Carrington asks Tommy to create a distraction at the Spand Express depot so he can get inside and destroy the company’s vans.

### Get the Coveralls from Rafael’s

Follow the shirt icon on your radar to Rafael’s clothes shop. Walk into the marker to change into the Coveralls. Then follow the next mission marker to Spand Express, where the workers are gathered outside the depot gate.

### Punch Four Different Workers to Open the Gate

Start a fight with one worker, then move on to the next. You need to engage at least four separate workers. If you’ve thrown several punches and the gate is still closed, switch targets. When the brawl starts, security opens the gate to deal with the crowd. Head inside toward the delivery vans.

### Destroy All Three Delivery Vans

Two vans are parked together beside an explosive canister. Back away and shoot the canister to take out both vans in one blast. The third van is parked separately. Drive it next to the second canister, get out, and back away before shooting. If you already have a shotgun, you can use it to destroy the last van directly.

The mission ends as soon as all three vans are destroyed. If Tommy is still on the mission after the first explosion, check the van parked on its own.

## Riot Mission Rewards and Unlocks

Completing Riot earns $1,000 and unlocks the Coveralls outfit. It also opens two story missions, Four Iron for Avery Carrington and Treacherous Swine for Colonel Cortez. In the Definitive Edition, completing Riot on a save eligible for trophies or achievements earns Legal Counsel.

## Final Thoughts

For free-roam chaos in GTA Vice City, make a manual save and type FIGHTFIGHTFIGHT on PC to start a pedestrian riot. When you’re done, reload your pre-cheat save to return to normal gameplay.

If you’re completing the Riot story mission, punch four different workers at the Spand Express depot to open the gate, then destroy all three delivery vans. Finishing the mission earns $1,000, unlocks the Coveralls outfit, and opens Four Iron and Treacherous Swine.
